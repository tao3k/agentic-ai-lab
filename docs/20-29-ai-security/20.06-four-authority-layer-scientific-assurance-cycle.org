* Decision

Agentic AI Lab assurance uses four correctness-authoritative layers:

1. *Normative layer* -- Org RFCs state the claim, assumptions, failure
   semantics, owner, and observable acceptance criteria.
2. *Formal layer* -- Lean states whether that claim follows from explicit
   assumptions, preserves counterexamples, and rejects an inconsistent model.
3. *Executable refinement layer* -- the POO-native Scheme reference transition
   and the Rust/Python SDK or runtime emit traces that refine the formal
   transition relation.
4. *Empirical qualification layer* -- red-team counterexamples, property and
   fuzz tests, differential runs, independent replay, fault injection, workload
   experiments, and operational observations qualify the executable claim in a
   declared environment.

The presentation layer is derived:

- Mermaid projects relationships and flows;
- Typst projects durable human-readable mathematics and reports.

Neither presentation form is a correctness authority. A diagram or rendered
formula cannot create a normative commitment, discharge a Lean theorem, prove
trace refinement, or qualify runtime behavior.

* Authority and feedback direction

The forward qualification path is:

#+begin_src text
Org claim -> Lean declaration -> Scheme/Rust/Python trace -> empirical receipt
#+end_src

The learning path runs in the opposite direction. An empirical counterexample
is retained as evidence and MUST reopen the affected executable, formal, and
normative claims until one of these explicit outcomes is published:

- the implementation is repaired while the claim and theorem remain valid;
- the formal assumptions or transition relation are refined;
- the RFC commitment is narrowed or replaced;
- the sample is shown inapplicable by independently replayable evidence.

An LLM detection hint, Mermaid revision, or Typst edit is not a valid closure
action.

* Cross-layer identity envelope

Every closure candidate MUST bind these identities in one evidence root:

- =rfcClaimId= and =rfcRevision=;
- =leanDeclarationSetId= and compiled theorem-set digest;
- =referenceTransitionId= for the POO-native Scheme semantics;
- =implementationArtifactId= for each Rust/Python implementation under test;
- =traceSchemaId= and =traceRoot=;
- =empiricalRunId=, environment identity, sample-set identity, and receipt root;
- presentation artifact identities, when present, as non-authoritative
  projections.

The normalized agent action/evidence envelope remains the observation boundary.
Provider-specific Cursor, Claude, Codex, or tool logs are adapters into that
envelope; Lean does not parse their native log formats.

* Closure states

Closure is reported per claim, never inferred from repository-wide success:

- =normative-only=: an RFC claim exists;
- =formally-closed=: the bound Lean declarations compile without prohibited
  proof fallback;
- =refinement-qualified=: bound executable traces satisfy the declared formal
  transition/refinement relation;
- =empirically-qualified=: the declared empirical campaign completed and its
  receipts replay independently;
- =four-layer-closed=: all four bindings hold and no applicable counterexample
  remains open;
- =unknown=: required evidence is missing or cannot be replayed;
- =reopened=: a new applicable counterexample invalidates a previous closure.

Passing Lean alone MUST NOT be reported as four-layer closure. Passing unit tests
alone MUST NOT be reported as executable refinement or empirical qualification.

* Executable refinement obligations

The executable layer is not ordinary implementation parity. For each accepted
transition, its trace MUST establish at least:

- pre-state and post-state identities;
- action, capability, tool, policy, and evidence-root bindings;
- formal transition or refinement-rule identity;
- contained effects and observed effect-domain coverage;
- deterministic normalization sufficient for independent replay;
- explicit unknown or fail-closed disposition when a required binding is absent.

The public construction surface remains POO-native and functional. Scheme owns
the reference semantic object/combinator surface; Rust and Python may implement
runtime and SDK refinements. Raw JSON is an evidence encoding, not the ordinary
module API.

* Empirical qualification obligations

The empirical layer answers questions that deductive proof alone does not:

- whether deployed adapters actually emit the assumed evidence;
- whether implementation traces conform under adversarial and malformed input;
- whether recovery, timeout, partial failure, concurrency, and replay behave as
  modeled;
- whether real workload distributions expose unknown regions or operational
  limits;
- whether a historical decision can be reproduced from its bundle without live
  provider state.

Every campaign declares its population or corpus, environment, configuration,
artifact digests, oracle, seeds where applicable, and known exclusions. Results
without those bindings are observations, not qualification receipts.

* Typed refinement and empirical admission contract

The formal executable-refinement boundary is not a loose collection of booleans.
For each candidate trace it binds:

- RFC claim and Lean declaration-set identities;
- formal transition identity;
- pre-state and post-state roots;
- normalized action, capability, and tool identities;
- policy and effect-domain roots;
- an evidence root whenever the formal transition requires evidence.

Any mismatch is an admission failure. Missing required evidence is =unknown= and
fails closed; it is not treated as a negative empirical sample or a successful
formal trace.

An empirical qualification receipt separately binds the executable trace root,
implementation artifact, environment, sample set, oracle, and run identity. It
is admitted only when independent replay succeeds and no applicable
counterexample remains open.

* Required metrics

Each claim family reports at least:

- =proofCoverage=: executions inside the formally modeled and proven region;
- =unknownEscalationRate=: executions that fail closed or require escalation
  because evidence or modeled coverage is insufficient;
- =replaySuccess=: historical decisions independently reproduced from bound
  bundles;
- =refinementCoverage=: executable transition kinds with verified formal-trace
  refinement;
- =counterexampleStatus=: open, repaired, excluded with evidence, or promoted to
  a versioned regression;
- empirical workload, latency, failure, and resource metrics relevant to the
  declared campaign.

Precision and recall may be reported for a detector, but cannot replace these
assurance metrics.

* Red-team-to-proof regression compilation

A high-value Explorer, ADR-Bench, or production counterexample SHOULD produce:

1. a minimized and versioned counterexample;
2. an affected RFC claim and assumption analysis;
3. a policy or semantic refinement when the old claim was incomplete;
4. a Lean theorem, countermodel, or property change;
5. a Scheme/Rust/Python executable regression trace;
6. an empirical replay bundle and versioned receipt.

The regression is closed only at the highest layer whose bound receipt exists.

* Presentation projection contract

Mermaid and Typst artifacts MUST reference stable claim, theorem, trace, and run
identities when depicting them. Generators SHOULD derive presentation artifacts
from authoritative metadata. A stale or divergent presentation is a reporting
defect; it does not mutate the underlying correctness state.

- normative: RFC commitments exist;

  yet reported.

not four-layer closed.

* Lean closure

The authority rules are modeled by:

- =AgenticAILabProof.Enterprise.AISecurityFourAuthorityLayerClosure=
- =AgenticAILabProof.Enterprise.AISecurityExecutableRefinementContractClosure=

The proof establishes that:

- full closure requires every authoritative layer;
- presentation evidence cannot substitute for any authoritative layer;
- an open applicable counterexample prevents full closure;
- four bound layers with no open counterexample are sufficient for the abstract
  closure predicate.

This is a proof about the closure criterion. It is not evidence that the pending
executable or empirical receipts already exist.

The typed refinement closure additionally proves that every cross-layer identity
mismatch and missing required evidence fails closed, and that replay failure or
an open applicable counterexample prevents empirical qualification.
