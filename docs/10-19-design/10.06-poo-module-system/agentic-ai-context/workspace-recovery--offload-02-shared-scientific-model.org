* Shared scientific model: structure, behavior, proof and explanation
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: recovery-scientific-model
:END:

The interrupted-write contract at the beginning of this document is the common
semantic reference. Each artifact has a distinct job; none substitutes for the
others or proves the whole system by itself.

| Concept | SysML design | Quint design module | Lean design namespace | D2 explanation |
|---------+--------------+---------------------+-----------------------+----------------|
| Same original question | RetainedRecoveryAttempt and ReconciliationResponsibility | Stable original-attempt/outcome key | outcomeDuty | One Attempt across all columns |
| Observed bytes | TemporalEvidenceLineageView | observeBytes | appearance_preserves_outcome | Added line, unknown writer |
| Exact admitted success | RecoveryClaimView and evidenceComparison.admitted | admitSuccess | admitted_success_resolves | Cut B: succeeded and resolved |
| Correction with or without surviving support | correctionComparison | correctAttribution, survivingSupportDeterminesDuty | copied_support_cannot_survive, independent_support_keeps_outcome_resolved | Same correction, two cut C outcomes |
| Competing explanations | RecoveryHypothesisView | Finite clue vocabulary only | Arbitrary clue strings, no causal truth oracle | File appearance leaves attribution open |
| Model freedom | ModelRecoveryExploration | Any enabled operation, including wait | InquiryTrace in arbitrary order | Example inquiries scoped to each cut |
| Independent dispatch guards | repeatJudgment and domainPolicy | Existing DurableRecoveryExtension module | eligibility_requires_both_guards, successful_effect_veto | Outside this focused evidence view |

[[file:workspace-recovery.qnt][RecoveryEvidenceInquiry]] is a bounded knowledge-change abstraction alongside the
existing dispatch model. Its nondeterministic action relation is a verification
vocabulary, not an Agent workflow. The three-revision bound and finite clue
names bound analysis only. They are not product limits or a closed exploration
API. =revise= assumes externally admitted, exact-effect evidence. It abstracts
Temporal source verification, digests, assessment methods and dependency closure;
it does not reimplement or prove them. Conflict blocks current success selection.
Correction may reopen the question while the earlier success remains history.

[[file:workspace-recovery.lean][The Lean companion]] states and proves properties of that abstract judgment:
observations, proposals and candidate findings preserve the outcome; arbitrary
inquiry traces preserve an initially unknown outcome; correction reopens its
question; revision retains previous cuts; successful effects veto eligibility;
and eligibility requires both current permission and safe repetition. The
booleans represent qualified propositions at an explicit cut, not ordinary
user-controlled feature switches. List membership in Lean corresponds to set
membership in Quint; ordering and duplicate proposal entries are not semantic
claims. A mechanically checked cross-language refinement is not supplied.

The earlier [[file:../../../../dev/d2/examples/recovery-evidence/case.ss][relationship view case data]] references this ORG and the existing SysML design through the
unchanged generic driver. Its inert POO presentation inputs choose local object
depictions and source-qualified explanatory relations. They introduce no Scheme
runtime implementation, new renderer, domain inference or per-case layout code.
=modules/d2= owns ASCENT selection, reader questions and layout constraints;
the existing SDK and ELK place objects and routes. Actual image readings are
retained in the companion =readings.ss= with the source cut and image digest.

The evidence diagram deliberately omits the dispatch mechanism to keep its
question readable. A diagram arrow denotes evidential dependence, a question
or feedback; it does not prescribe execution order. Actual source authenticity,
full Temporal admission, dependency completeness, causal identification and
runtime liveness remain outside the finite abstraction. No proof of reader
understanding or real-world effect completion follows from a rendered image.


** One Attempt across three evidence cuts

The interrupted write left the expected line in the file. At cut A, the
observation establishes the bytes and leaves the original effect's outcome open.
At cut B, the result owner supplies receipt R1 and its use is admitted as exact
support for this Attempt's effect. Context selects success and ASCENT resolves
that outcome duty. At cut C, admitted correction R2 attributes R1 to another
Attempt. R1 was the sole qualifying support in this example. Context returns to
unknown and ASCENT reopens the same Task owner's duty, keyed by Attempt,
question and scope. Cut B still records what the admitted evidence established
at B. The file observation also survives the correction.

| Cut | SysML evidenceComparison | Quint | Lean | Suggested model inquiry |
|-----+--------------------------+-------+------+-------------------------|
| A | initial | observeBytes leaves outcome unknown | appearance_preserves_outcome | Locate a receipt source or examine another writer |
| B | admitted | admitSuccess resolves duties | admitted_success_resolves | Optionally inspect claims that use the result |
| C | corrected | correctAttribution restores the same duty and retains B | attributed_success_then_correction | Compare Attempt identities or seek an independent effect record |

The cut labels are the evidence history selected for this example. The open
model can investigate alternatives in any order, wait, or use newly admitted
material. Its proposed inquiries have no admission or dispatch authority.
With independent success support after R2, the evaluator reassesses the claim
on that basis. Unrelated corrections leave the original result support intact.

** One correction, two surviving evidence sets
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: recovery-correction-surviving-support
:END:

Keep the original Attempt and exact effect from the interrupted write. Both
alternatives start with a successful judgment at B. In both, the source owner's
admitted correction R2 changes the attribution of R1. R1-copy is a derived use
of the receipt, even if another system stores it or renders it differently.
Temporal uses dependency lineage to withdraw both R1 and R1-copy from current
support. Their versions remain in history.

W1 represents a separate workspace write record that was independently admitted
for the same Attempt and exact effect. Independence is relative to the corrected
premise. If W1's attribution uses a join, assertion or copied identifier that
ultimately relies on the corrected R1 attribution, W1 also belongs in the
affected set. A different source name or matching file bytes cannot establish
independence. The two examples assume no unresolved competing admission.

| At cut C | R1 and its copy were the only support | Independently admitted W1 also existed |
|----------+---------------------------------------+----------------------------------------|
| Temporal | Withdraw both active uses; keep history | Withdraw the same uses; W1 survives |
| Context | Unknown for the same effect | Succeeded on W1 under the current cut |
| ASCENT original outcome question | Reopen the existing Attempt/question/scope key | Keep that key resolved |
| ASCENT review scope | Flag claims citing affected uses and their dependent conclusions | Same scope; a surviving outcome does not validate every downstream claim |
| Model example inquiry | Compare Attempt identities or seek an independent write record | Check which affected claims W1 actually supports and seek separate evidence where useful |

The design separates outcome reconciliation from review of a withdrawn basis.
An affected claim may still stand on other admitted support, may require new
evidence or may remain unresolved. ASCENT can derive affected candidates from
the scoped dependency graph. Incomplete dependency information limits any
claim that the affected set is complete. Reassessment and investigation can
be combined, deferred or pursued concurrently.

In SysML, =correctionComparison.copiedOnly= and =independentSurvives= hold
the two readings. Quint's =correctAttribution= subtracts the uses reached from R1's corrected
attribution from =successUses= and exposes the withdrawn premise set as
=affectedUseReview=. The graph traversal is modeled over the finite dependency graph below.
Authenticating and admitting W1 remain assumptions at this boundary. Lean checks the bounded cases with
=copied_support_cannot_survive= and
=independent_support_keeps_outcome_resolved=. The independent use membership
lemma covers arbitrary lists of the three modeled use kinds. Historical
success is represented by its prior cut; the archive itself belongs to Temporal.

** A separate source can inherit the same attribution
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: recovery-indirect-attribution
:END:

W1 records a workspace write under a storage-local identifier. To cite it as
proof for the original Attempt, the evaluator joins that identifier through
an Attempt crosswalk. The crosswalk was derived from R1's attribution. W1
therefore has two required premises: the raw write record and its binding to
this exact Attempt and effect. The raw record comes from a separate system.
The effect-specific use of it still depends on R1.

At cut C, the source owner's admitted R2 targets the versioned R1 attribution.
The evidence view follows premise-to-dependent edges from that seed. It
reaches the R1 receipt use and the crosswalk. The crosswalk reaches the W1
attribution, which reaches the W1 success use. Both active success uses at B
are affected. The traversal also reaches B's successful outcome and the
recovery summary that cited it. It leaves the W1 raw record untouched. The
R1-copy node remains in the shared example graph and is reached if present;
it was not an active use in this branch.

| Status of the exact W1 use at C | Required basis | Decision |
|---------------------------------+----------------+----------|
| Affected | A recorded path from the corrected premise reaches the W1 use | Withdraw it from current support |
| Pending | No such path is recorded, and dependency coverage for this use or its binding is incomplete | Keep it out of current success support and derive a binding question |
| Independent for this correction | Exact effect binding and use are admitted at C, with adequate scoped lineage coverage and no path from the corrected premise | Retain W1 as support under the applicable conflict checks |

An independence claim is relative to the corrected premise, the exact use
version, its claim and the selected cut. Checking only a producer name,
a raw record hash or a missing graph edge cannot establish it. Reachability
identifies judgments whose basis must be reviewed. Reassessment can produce
the same verdict when another independent basis survives. Past verdicts
and records remain available at their original cuts.

At C in this case, no valid success support remains. Context records an
unknown outcome for the same effect. ASCENT reopens the original outcome
question for its Task owner, derives a separate W1 binding question for an
evidence custodian or scoped investigator, and flags the reached judgments
for review. The dependency path provides the reason for each request. The
model may inspect the crosswalk provenance, look for an authenticated direct
binding between the storage identifier and the Attempt, or seek a separately
admitted write record. A candidate investigation result can inform admission
through Temporal. Neither the candidate nor an ASCENT question authorizes
another write. These inquiries may be combined, deferred or run concurrently.

| Artifact | Correspondence |
|----------+----------------|
| SysML | =indirectAttribution= declares the premise edges, prior judgments and responsibilities |
| Quint | =impact= computes finite reachability; =correctAttribution= partitions current uses; =bindingReview= and =judgmentReview= expose obligations |
| Lean | =indirect_binding_reopens_outcome= checks the dependent branch; =missing_lineage_cannot_supply_success= checks incomplete coverage; =correction_preserves_raw_w1_record= distinguishes the observation from its use |
| D2 | The graph draws the active case, with the old judgments at B and the current response at C |

The Quint and Lean abstractions share a nine-vertex graph. Their bounded
expansion follows each recorded premise edge to its dependents. The
verification assumes a trustworthy scoped inventory of admitted edges and
uses. It checks the decisions for the modeled independent, indirect and
unverified lineage states. The abstraction keeps the original versions and models exploration after
correction. The next section adds a separate admission judgment for a new use
version.

** Readmitting W1 on an independent binding
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: recovery-w1-readmission
:END:

At C the evaluator still has the immutable raw W1 record, but its former use
W1-v1 depends on the corrected R1 attribution. The model discovers D1, an
assertion from the storage authority directly binding the original Attempt and
append effect to storage write 42 and the retained W1 record digest. D1 enters
as a candidate. Context stays unknown and the outcome and attribution questions
remain open until Temporal selects a qualified use.

| Check | What the authorized evaluator establishes |
|-------+--------------------------------------------|
| Exact subject | D1 identifies the original durable Attempt and the exact append effect, including the effect's target and intent |
| Exact occurrence | D1 names the same storage-scoped write identity and immutable record digest found in W1. The scoped identity includes the storage namespace or epoch needed to avoid collisions |
| Committed effect | The retained W1 record and admitted contract establish this write's commit and that it realizes the intended effect. Matching file content alone supplies no commit or identity proof |
| Authority and lineage | D1's issuer is authorized to attest this binding. The candidate, verification basis and required premises have adequate scoped coverage. Neither the binding nor its verified effect use relies on the corrected R1 attribution |
| Current use assessment | The verdict covers this exact candidate version, W1 record version and effect claim at the admission cut. If that cut advances before selection, verify again against the new basis |

Temporal selects W1-v2 as a distinct use of the same immutable raw record,
with D1 and the fresh assessment as its basis. W1-v2 references W1-v1 for
version history. That reference carries no evidential authority. Selection is
conditional on the assessment's basis cut still being current. If identity,
authority, completeness, commit or independence cannot be established, W1-v2
cannot supply active success support. The model can seek other evidence or
defer. These checks form a conjunction over admitted facts. No prescribed
order is required for their discovery.

| Scope | At C after R2 | At D with W1-v2 |
|-------+---------------+-----------------|
| Context | Outcome unknown | Succeeded on the new basis if the result is uncontested; otherwise the outcome remains open |
| ASCENT binding responsibility | Confirm whether W1 belongs to the original effect | Close the same binding question when W1-v2 is selected |
| ASCENT outcome responsibility | Reconcile the original Attempt and effect | Close the same outcome question once Context selects uncontested success |
| ASCENT downstream review | Review B's outcome and summary | The new outcome answers the effect question. Review of summary-B's other claims remains open until each affected claim has a current basis |
| Temporal history | R1, R2, W1-v1, B's success and C's unknown are retained | Add D1, its assessment, W1-v2 and D's judgment. Past evidence, withdrawal reasons and judgments remain queryable at their original cuts |

| Artifact | Correspondence |
|----------+----------------|
| SysML | =directBindingAdmission= names the candidate, checks, new use, historical links and the three responsibility scopes |
| Quint | =discoverBinding= records a candidate; =qualifies= checks exact identity and basis; =readmitW1= selects a distinct use; =bindingReview=, =duties= and =judgmentReview= project current responsibilities |
| Lean | =AdmissionGate= states the verified conditions; =checked_direct_binding_can_admit= constructs a valid gate for D1; the readmission theorems prove conditional success, distinct closure and history retention |
| D2 | The figure compares the two independent source references, the candidate and its assessment, and the current response at C and D |

The model represents verified authority, lineage and commit checks as facts
supplied by an authorized Temporal evaluator. It does not authenticate storage
records or prove completeness of a real evidence inventory. Quint uses
bounded alternative candidate and assessment records and a single new version.
The key =W1= in the original graph denotes W1-v1. The separate
=admittedBasis= records the assessment that selected W1-v2. An unresolved
result conflict can coexist with a verified binding to the exact effect.
Lean uses the same fixture identities with an explicit gate proof. The graph
of the B-to-C correction continues to describe the old versions. W1-v2's
proof basis is represented separately.

** Reviewing each claim in the recovery summary
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: recovery-summary-claim-review
:END:

The design now gives the previously abstract summary-B a three-claim
inventory. These are illustrative claims for this case. Each old citation
keeps its original version and basis, including any use of the superseded
Attempt mapping. At D, the original append effect has an uncontested
success judgment on W1-v2. That judgment supplies a possible basis for
one summary claim. Temporal assesses the exact use against the exact
claim version before it becomes a citation in the new summary.

| Stable claim key | Claim in summary B | Required current basis | ASCENT responsibility |
|------------------+--------------------+------------------------+-----------------------|
| =append-committed= | The original append effect committed | W1-v2 and a fresh assessment for the same Attempt, effect and claim digest | Close this claim's review when the assessment is admitted |
| =single-commit= | Exactly one commit for this effect in the recovery window | An independently scoped storage audit with complete coverage and one matching committed occurrence | Keep open until the audit proves the scoped count |
| =no-replay-dispatch= | No replay dispatch for this effect in the recovery window | An independently scoped dispatch audit covering all relevant dispatchers and recording zero matching replay dispatches | Keep open until the audit proves the scoped absence |

The recovery window is the declared interval of this case. The audits bind
its endpoints, the original Attempt and exact effect, and the identities
needed to reconcile occurrences across the relevant stores or dispatchers.
A complete storage count can coexist with a replay that never committed.
A dispatch audit and a storage audit therefore answer different questions.
Incomplete, unverified or conflicting audit material leaves the corresponding
claim unresolved in this design slice.

ASCENT derives one open review per unchecked claim key. The key is scoped
to the summary lineage, so replacing a citation can discharge the same
question without duplicating it. The model may inspect coverage, compare
audit identity mappings, prepare a scoped draft, or defer. Claims can be
verified in any order. Drafting or publishing a summary leaves claim
responsibilities unchanged.

The selected publication policy for this example allows a partial revision
containing the verified append claim. It carries every currently checked
claim as an assertion and names every other manifest claim as unresolved.
For example, with only the append claim checked, the partial revision
asserts that claim and lists the count and dispatch questions separately.
A full revision requires all three claims and can be admitted directly.
If an audit contradicts a historical claim, a revised claim or explicit
retraction requires its own assessment. The next section models a refuted historical claim and a scoped rewrite.

At selection, Temporal checks the candidate version and predecessor,
its current evidence cut and review revision, and the exact correspondence
between assertions and verified claim bases. It verifies the unresolved
inventory and the coverage label against the manifest. The checks are
conjunctive and depend on the facts available at selection. An assessment
change invalidates an older draft until it is re-evaluated. Admission appends
an immutable version with its own references. The historical B payload and
its citations remain queryable as of B. A partial version can satisfy
publication for its declared scope while the aggregate review remains open.
The aggregate review closes when all claim checks are satisfied and the
full current revision is admitted.

| Artifact | Correspondence |
|----------+----------------|
| SysML | =summaryReview= separates the three claim bases, the candidate selection and the historical predecessor |
| Quint | =assessSummaryClaim= admits exact per-claim bases; =admitSummary= selects the current manifest and records predecessor and version history |
| Lean | =w1_cannot_verify_audit_claim=, =other_claim_review_unchanged=, =admitted_summary_assertions_and_open_items_exact= and the partial/full review theorems check the separation and closure conditions |
| D2 | The figure aligns each B claim with its required current basis and ASCENT review, then contrasts the two admissible scopes |

The finite models hold the recovered effect cut fixed and add assessments
bound to that cut. The review revision distinguishes successive inventories
of checked claims. In a broader system, an advance of the Temporal evidence
cut requires new current assessments. Evidence authority, audit coverage
and content digests enter as verified facts. The archive represents B by its
immutable reference and retains the full modeled records of later publications.
Physical archival and authentication remain outside the abstraction.

** A replay was dispatched and only one write committed
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: recovery-summary-audit-refutation
:END:

The current Context outcome still succeeds on W1-v2. A complete storage audit
confirms one commit for the original effect in the recovery window. The dispatch
journal supplies a matching replay event in that window. Dispatch and commit
are different observations. One replay dispatch and one commit can coexist.

| Claim from B | Verified basis in this case | ASCENT and the revised summary |
|--------------+-----------------------------+--------------------------------|
| Original append committed | W1-v2 with a current assessment of this exact claim | Keep the claim. Close the review as supported. |
| Exactly one commit | The complete storage audit counts one matching commit in scope | Keep the claim. Close the review as supported. |
| No replay dispatch | An admitted matching replay event contradicts zero | Close the fact review as refuted. Disclose the withdrawal in the revision. |
| Exactly one replay dispatch | Complete dispatch coverage and a separate assessment of this new claim digest | Include the rewrite if verified. Otherwise publish the withdrawal on its own. |

A single admitted event disproves zero. An exact-one rewrite additionally
requires complete coverage of the relevant dispatchers, the same recovery
window and an independently verified effect binding. The diagram shows the
observed replay count as a witness to refutation. Its count does not assert
coverage for an exact-one replacement. The same audit can supply separate
assessments for the old and new claim digests.

ASCENT closes a fact review with a supported or refuted disposition. It keeps
the summary publication duty open until Temporal selects a qualified revision.
The selected draft binds the current cut, review revision, parent version and
exact claim assessments. It asserts only supported claims, lists refuted claims
as withdrawals, and lists unassessed claims as unresolved. A rewrite requires
disclosure of the old claim's withdrawal. Full accounting for B can include
a withdrawal without an exact replacement. Optional investigations and draft
proposals can proceed independently.

The new summary adds an immutable version and a predecessor reference to B.
Summary B and its old evidence remain queryable at their original cut. The
new version does not give the old zero-replay assertion current authority.

| Artifact | Correspondence |
|----------+----------------|
| SysML | =auditRefutation= identifies the two audits, the retained claims, the refutation and the conditional rewrite |
| Quint | =replayRefutation= records a positive witness against absence; =positiveClaims= and =refutedClaims= separate assertions and withdrawals; =admitSummary= checks coverage and preserves the archive |
| Lean | =replay_refutation_closes_review_and_records_withdrawal=, =exact_replay_rewrite_requires_complete_coverage= and =replay_rewrite_requires_disclosed_withdrawal= check the distinct conditions |
| D2 ASCENT | The evidence and revision objects are selected and composed by the existing D2 module. Pictograms carry the two counts and the dispositions. |

The model holds the effect cut fixed and accepts one admitted disposition per
claim version. The hypothetical zero-event audit and the witnessed replay
branch represent alternative qualified observations. Reassessment of a later
correction to an already current audit requires a new cut. Audit authenticity
and coverage enter this design as authorized facts.

** Closing dispatch coverage for an exact replay count
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: recovery-dispatch-coverage
:END:

The fixture has two dispatchers, A and B. The authority attests the dispatcher
inventory for the original Attempt, exact append effect and half-open recovery
window. An admitted manifest fixes the required ranges A/early, A/late,
B/early and B/late. Its real counterpart includes dispatcher identities,
namespace and generation, and routing or ownership changes inside the window.
The observed log sources cannot define the expected inventory.

A qualified range binds that manifest scope and current evidence cut. Its
authorized issuer seals the range with the applicable opening and terminal
fences. It accounts for all sequence positions in the range, including a
certified empty range, and reconciles duplicate deliveries or conflicting
versions. A missing range, stale epoch, gap, unsealed end or unresolved
conflict keeps coverage open. The finite model represents each verified range
by one qualified slot receipt and uses a fixed authoritative manifest. An
empty event field represents verified absence inside a sealed range.

| View | What the evidence establishes | ASCENT and publication |
|------+-------------------------------+------------------------|
| A seen, B missing | A has an admitted replay event with ID replay-42. B has no qualified ranges yet. | Close the zero-replay fact review as refuted. Keep coverage and exact-count reviews open. A withdrawal revision may be admitted. |
| A and B covered | The four distinct required slots are sealed for the same scope and cut. The union contains one distinct matching dispatch ID. | Close the coverage review. Close the exact-count review on admission of its own assessment. A revision may assert exactly one replay. |

An admitted positive event is sufficient to refute zero. Exact cardinality
requires an admitted complete manifest, a unique qualified receipt for every
required range, and deduplication by authoritative dispatch identity. A
forwarded copy of replay-42 counts once. A distinct replay-43 makes the
exact-one claim fail even when coverage is complete. The storage commit count
remains a separate judgment.

ASCENT can ask for B's missing ranges, an authority-backed inventory, a
watermark or reconciliation evidence. The investigation and draft choices
remain independent where their premises allow. Once the coverage basis is
complete, assessment checks the exact replay claim against the distinct
event count. The summary draft binds that assessment, the manifest and
range receipts, the current cut and review revision, and its predecessor.
An older draft cannot silently inherit a new count.

The illustrated path admits a withdrawal revision v2 while coverage is open.
It then admits v3 with the exact-one claim after the separate assessment.
B and the prior v2 remain queryable with their own bases. A design can also
admit the exact revision directly when its complete basis is already available.

| Artifact | Correspondence |
|----------+----------------|
| SysML | =dispatchCoverage= compares the two coverage cuts and preserves the version relation |
| Quint | =admitDispatchManifest= and =admitDispatchRow= qualify evidence; =completeDispatchCoverage= checks all slots and =replayIds= deduplicates; =dispatchBasesAllowed= separates witness and exact gates |
| Lean | =witnessed_replay_refutes_without_full_coverage=, =missing_b_late_blocks_exact_replay=, =complete_coverage_and_one_id_allow_exact_replay= and =two_distinct_replays_block_exact_one= check the scoped admission distinction |
| D2 ASCENT | A and B use matching log-band objects to contrast missing and sealed coverage. The summary stack depicts the selected example history. |

Range authentication, gap-free sealing, dispatcher inventory authority and
canonical event identities are verified inputs. The bounded model compares
coverage within one recovered effect cut as qualified receipts arrive. A
later conflicting correction belongs to a new cut. Published revisions
retain their exact manifest and range snapshot in the model.

** A previously sealed dispatch range is corrected
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: recovery-dispatch-seal-correction
:END:

At the prior dispatch cut, summary v3 asserted exactly one replay on the
complete manifest and all four sealed ranges. Later the dispatch authority
admits a correction that withdraws B/late's seal because it omitted a sequence
interval. The correction names the precise version and scope it affects.
The current dispatch selection is cleared as the new cut is opened. The old
snapshot and its basis remain in history. Context's separate success judgment
for the committed append still has its original write evidence.

| Current evidence | Current dispatch claim | ASCENT responsibility |
|------------------+------------------------+-----------------------|
| Seal revoked, A not yet requalified | No current replay assertion selected | Reopen coverage and exact-count review |
| A's witness requalified, B gap remains | At least one replay can be assessed and selected | Keep coverage and exact count open; no zero-replay assertion follows from the gap |
| Fresh B seal, full manifest, one distinct ID | Exact one can be selected after a fresh assessment | Close coverage and then resolve the exact-count question on its basis |
| Fresh B seal also reveals replay-43 | The exact-one gate refuses; an exact-two claim needs its own assessment | Close coverage and keep count review open in this bounded exact-one model |

The original exact-one assessment belonged to the original dispatch cut.
No old log row becomes current by changing its label. Each carried range
needs explicit requalification against the new cut and scope, or equivalent
admitted evidence of unchanged applicability. The new B receipt must cover
the corrected interval and complete the authoritative inventory. Counting
deduplicates by event identity across qualified ranges. Independent
requalification, manifest checks and candidate drafting can proceed in any
available order.

The active summary pointer loses its exact assertion on correction. A new
withdrawal or lower-bound revision can be selected on its current basis;
a later exact revision requires both complete coverage and a new exact
assessment. Each admission adds an immutable snapshot. B, the withdrawal
version and v3 remain queryable as of their own cuts. The prior exact wording
has no fallback authority while the current basis is open.

| Artifact | Correspondence |
|----------+----------------|
| SysML | =dispatchSealCorrection= separates the correction, the requalified witness, current duties and the historical exact snapshot |
| Quint | =DispatchSealRevision= reuses the original coverage and identity functions, models separate requalification, assessment and selection, and retains the previous snapshot |
| Lean | =correction_retires_selection_and_preserves_history=, =old_row_cannot_be_carried_without_current_qualification=, =witness_can_support_lower_bound_while_exact_is_blocked= and the reseal theorems check the gate distinctions |

The abstraction holds the Attempt, effect and window fixed. Its dispatch cut
advances independently of the effect outcome. Authorization of the correction,
new seal, inventory and requalification are verified inputs. The fixture
models one correction. The two-ID branch blocks exact one. The next section
adds the separate assessment and publication gate for exact two.

** Admitting exactly two replays after the corrected seal
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: recovery-dispatch-exact-two
:END:

** Replay count: one question, evidence that can change the answer
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: recovery-dispatch-count-mvp

Context keeps one question keyed by the original Attempt, append effect
and occurrence window: how many distinct dispatches replayed this effect?

a record; a canonical dispatch identity names the event it describes.

| Evidence | What the same question can say |
