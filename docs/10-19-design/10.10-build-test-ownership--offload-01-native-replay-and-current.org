* OpenTofu deployment qualification

The functional gate compares each cold build with its selected Make spec,
requires zero compilation on B's hot build, and checks that cleaning A leaves
B's hashes intact. The child must use B's products, execute the business
=.get=, and exit successfully. Before the first access the scope remains cold.
The owner-qualified scope counters require four scope constructions on that
access and zero on the repeat. These assertions apply on both platforms.

The 120-second reproduction deadline measures completion of the entire
qualification. Use the phase receipts to compare performance on the same OS
and Gerbil toolchain with the same installed prerequisites. In particular,
report cold Make, first business =.get=, and time from the child's =OK= to its
natural exit separately. Darwin's dynamic image mapping and unloading can
affect both runtime measurements, and preinstalled Core modules change the
number of Make targets. A 71-target result cannot qualify the 88-target run.
