* Orthogonal runtime control axes without collapsing Context, Session or Sandbox

Scheduled ORG =Agenda= triggers, independent =Session= retry scheduling, and
=Sandbox= isolation regularly collapse into implicit privilege escalation when
an overdue timer is mistaken for execution permission, a crashed provider call
is blindly retried, or a sandbox allows outbound network egress while holding
both untrusted external input and restricted private data. Building on
[[file:13-runtime-background-clock.org][Slice 13]], [[file:14-runtime-durable-recovery.org][Slice 14]], [[file:org-durable-declarations.org][ORG Durable Declarations]],
[[file:35-org-context-modules.org][Slice 35]] and [[file:40-context-governance-boundary.org][Slice 40]], this slice formalizes the joint
governance invariant across those three runtime axes. No Scheme sources are
modified.

* Architecture: Alarm Clock, Foggy-Crash Scout and Locked Carrier-Pigeon Cage

| Control axis | Semantic owner | Operation and capability | Governance invariant |
|--------------+----------------+--------------------------+----------------------|
| 1. Durable Agenda & Watch Wakes | =ORG Durable Agenda= | =SCHEDULED= / =DEADLINE= rows and evidence watches emit due occurrence wakes | =WakeCarriesExecutionAuthority = false=; every wake re-checks live source cut and Cedar/runtime grants at the handoff fence |
| 2. Crash-Ambiguous Attempt Fencing | Independent =Session= (=Task= / =Attempt= / =Turn=) | Separates consumed handoff fences from observed provider receipts; runs read-only reconciliation probes after crashes | =outcome = unknownAfterCrash= blocks non-idempotent redispatch until attested final non-acceptance (=confirmedNoSend=), and any retry strictly advances =attemptGen= |
| 3. Sandbox Intersection & Lethal-Trifecta Lock | =Context= $\times$ =Sandbox= Governance Gate | Intersects Context capability bounds with the selected Sandbox profile allowlist | $\mathrm{Cap}_{\mathrm{eff}} \subseteq \mathrm{Cap}_{\mathrm{ctx}} \cap \mathrm{Cap}_{\mathrm{sandbox}}$, and $(\mathrm{UntrustedInput} \land \mathrm{PrivateData} \land \neg \mathrm{EndorsedEgress}) \implies \neg \mathrm{ExternalEgress}$ |
