=ContextSession.qnt= checks the original two-session/two-worker lifecycle with
one turn, revision cap four and source cuts zero/one. Receipts retain the
proposal revision, turn, cut and authorization. Closed sessions stay closed.

=WorktreeContext.qnt= checks two worktrees, two sessions/workers and revision
cap two. Imports retain both cuts, proposal revision, authorizations and
applicability. Reads retain owner scope and authorization. Both safe instances
and all ten original broken guard cases are in the shared executable catalog.
Run =just check-session-context-quint=; =just check-session-proof= also runs the
separate Lean Session target. These are finite abstract transitions, not a
proof of every concrete runtime interleaving.
