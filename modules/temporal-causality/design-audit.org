* Scope and evidence boundary

This record resolves the engine design choices left open across
[[file:framework.org][the semantic framework]], RFC 161 and RFC 45-70/45-72/45-73.
It audits source at Agentic AI Lab =3101deb8b0ac2b99d01840ac9b04e075aff442e4=.
The decisions below are normative for subsequent implementation. A decision
being accepted does not mean its executable gate has passed.

The current reusable Scheme engine has finite hypothesis classification,
explicit time, immutable revisions, reverse dependency planning and inert
conclusion selection. At the audited base head, the C/Python ABI =temporal.solve= instead
invokes =gerbil-ascent/temporal/lens= through =src/ffi/semantic.ss=.
Those are distinct supported profiles with distinct inputs and receipts.
The ABI tool roundtrip receipt tests that lens, its binding and model result
consumption. It does not qualify hypothesis-family execution, Quint behavior
refinement, clock authenticity or active conclusion publication.

No external research results are requalified by this source audit. The paper
map in RFC 161 remains research context. No new test pass is claimed here.

* D1: Select an explicit semantic profile before classifying

The basic engine profile is =finite-hypothesis-family=: a nonempty declared
candidate set, immutable observations, explicit constraints and exclusive or
overlapping family mode. =Possible= means the target passes the supported
known constraints. =Refuted= means a known constraint on that target fails;
this local refutation does not require exploring every other candidate.
=Necessary= means the target is the sole admissible candidate in an exhausted,
caller-declared complete exclusive family with no unknown candidates.
It is a family-relative uniqueness judgment. Overlapping families never
obtain necessity from uniqueness under this checker.

A future =finite-behavior-space= profile must supply an interpretation of
worlds/traces, interventions and observations. In that profile, possible
requires a satisfying witness; necessary requires nonempty admissible worlds,
exhaustion and universal satisfaction; refuted requires exhaustive absence
of a satisfying world. An inconsistent or empty admissible behavior space
must have an explicit diagnostic and cannot yield vacuous necessity.

The profile and family mode belong in semantic identity. Candidate receipts
cannot be relabeled as world/trace receipts. The existing Schema remains the
public finite-family contract; an explicit profile discriminator is required
before introducing a second interpretation on the same dispatch surface.

* D2: Keep truth scope, evidence coverage and execution budget separate

Completeness has three different subjects: the hypothesis vocabulary, the
source observation inventory, and the explored search frontier. Exhausting
one does not close the others. A current =family-complete?= value is a caller
assumption about alternatives. A watermark or coverage digest is a declared
reference until its source owner verifies it. Neither establishes empirical
causation or source completeness merely by appearing in a POO object.

An admitted closed-absence or finality claim must bind source set, partition,
sequence range/gaps, cut, generation, valid-time projection, policy revision,
late-event policy, issuer and the verifier's exact receipt. Verification must
replay the claim and confirm issuer authority for that scope. Changing scope,
generation, policy or receipt invalidates reuse. Basic pure family queries
remain available with explicit assumptions and open coverage.

Execution limits live in the query and execution receipt, outside model
identity. Cancellation, deadline, memory cap or solver interruption produces
an incomplete execution with its frontier. Such interruption cannot supply
exhaustion. A previously verified positive witness may still support possible;
a negative result needs its profile's required closure. Five seconds without
genuine provider content remains a failed real-model acceptance attempt;
heartbeat logs and private reasoning do not satisfy that progress condition.

* D3: Admit clock conversion through an explicit verified relation

The initial profile compares coordinates only inside one declared clock
domain. Distinct domains remain incomparable; absent or declared coordinates
remain unknown. Event time, ingress time, admission time, processing time,
logical generation and monotonic duration must remain separate axes.

The extension boundary is a POO conversion relation binding source and target
domains, issuer/verifier evidence, validity range, uncertainty enclosure and
revision. It maps an input interval to an output enclosure rather than silently
choosing a point. A definite ordering is permitted only when every coordinate
in the admitted enclosure satisfies it. No mapping is inferred from equal
numbers, similar clock names or a shared clock role. Cyclic or competing
conversions cannot choose a favorable path: inconsistent enclosures block
admission, and insufficient evidence preserves unknown/incomparable status.
Clock-source authenticity belongs to the source owner; Temporal replays the
verified relation. The current engine has no such conversion adapter.

* D4: Keep provider assertions inert until independently admitted

GQL, ASCENT and model Providers return candidate material for the same exact
cut and generation. Candidate exchange reviewability is a readiness state.
A local row check establishes row/count/digest consistency, while derivation
correctness, source coverage and verifier authority are separate obligations.
An opaque proof ID, valid check symbol or model confidence cannot fulfill them.

Before conclusion admission, the semantic owner must verify materialized
result bytes, supported proof method, exact query/policy/source binding and
checker authority. The verification result has its own immutable identity
and declared claim class: structural dependency, temporal consistency,
family-relative classification, or intervention-qualified causal claim.
Unsupported claim classes remain candidates. MRR owns live result transport;
Temporal must consume its original result admission receipt without inventing
query, parser or source identities. Raw model output cannot define a new
hypothesis mechanism or grant effect authority.

* D5: Bind Quint to a staged source closure and named interpretation

The maintained source inventory is =packages/proofs/quint/*.qnt=. The shared
checker snapshots the local modules before verification, selects named safety
and temporal properties and binds all source hashes and exact tool versions.
Changing an import changes that inventory identity. Missing imports, wrong
version, unexpected counterexample, interruption or a nonempty final frontier
fails admission. The five-second no-output and 45-second case limits apply
independently of dependency preparation.

The inert Scheme v1 receipt establishes finite behavioral evidence. The native
POO semantic model remains the owner of source/cut/authority identity. A general
projection correspondence needs a separate interpretation and theorem. Formal
checks are explicit assurance gates and do not execute during pure query use.

* D6: Reconcile late evidence through immutable revisions

Keep the canonical evidence journal as replay authority. A late assertion,
correction or retraction creates a new admission-time revision and projection;
it never rewrites the historical cut. A change in valid-time query is a
separate reprojection trigger even when the admission cut is unchanged.
Without an admitted parameter-independence proof, reevaluate the entire
indexed scope. A partial reverse index cannot certify an unaffected result.

Jointly reevaluate affected nonmonotone SCCs. Stabilization must retain its
premises, rule/policy revision, scope and proof receipt. Oscillation or resource
exhaustion yields suspension; no transient candidate becomes active. Competing
correction branches remain conflicted until an explicit authority-backed
resolution. A source watermark violation preserves the old scoped receipt,
withdraws its current applicability and triggers the late-event policy on a
new revision; it cannot retroactively turn an open claim into closed absence.
This decision specifies reconciliation; current revision/index helpers do not
implement source authentication or general SCC evaluation.

* D7: Publish through one runtime transaction and replayable recovery

Scheme owns immutable conclusions and CAS preparation. Runtime owns the
external pointer and authorized transaction. The library exposes a POO-native
commit request projection; C/Python consume that projection without creating
a second semantic contract. Read-only =temporal.solve= is not a commit API.

A future commit must bind subject/scope, admitted conclusion/proof, canonical
journal, cut/projection, policy/generation, expected pointer version and
predecessor, current authorization evidence and idempotency identity. Its
linearization point must atomically validate those preconditions, select the
revision/tombstone, increment the version and retain a recoverable transition
receipt. If authorization is issued outside the store, its revision/fence must
be validated at this point; a preflight permit alone does not close the race.
An adapter unable to enforce this contract must reject publication.

A stale pointer or authorization fence yields conflict and requires fresh
observation and reevaluation. No automatic last-write-wins or blind retry.
Retrying the same idempotency identity must return the original committed
receipt or recover its outcome, never perform a second selection. A crash
between selection and client response must be recoverable by that identity.
External business effects and compensation are separate authorized operations
with their own receipts; pointer selection cannot claim to undo past effects.
Runtime C and Python must demonstrate the same transaction contract before
this engine is advertised as supporting authorized publication.

* D8: Add behavior and intervention support behind a separate admission gate

The reusable basic profile does not infer mechanisms. A behavior model must
compose POO-native variables/domains, supported mechanism families,
environment constraints, observation map, intervention and query obligations.
An intervention binds model revision, onset, target variables, replacement
mechanism/policy, horizon and assumptions. Unsupported mechanism semantics
fail admission; stochastic Providers additionally bind estimand, support,
uncertainty and method evidence. A source fork or numerical trajectory contrast
alone cannot establish an actual cause or an individual counterfactual.

Safety, reachability and progress are distinct property values. Progress must
state fairness and environment assumptions; a safety pass does not imply
progress. The first behavior slice must reuse one core API for two independent
vertical vocabularies with no edits to core for the second. Public raw =Next=
lambdas, record/alist DSLs and hard-coded Healthcare actions are rejected.

* Delivery order and exit criteria

1. Preserve the existing pure finite-family and lens profiles. Link every
   public entrypoint to its exact profile, supported claims and receipt.
2. Close D2/D4 evidence admission and D6 revision applicability before using
   derived conclusions for external selection. A verifier must expose which
   obligation it discharged; a digest-only attestation is insufficient.
3. Implement D7 on Runtime C/Python with race and crash recovery evidence.
   This is the next publication slice; no vertical business adapter is needed
   to test it. Existing generic effect machinery may be reused only after its
   projection is shown to satisfy this Temporal transaction contract.
4. Extend D5 frozen qualification and D3 verified conversions as separate
   slices. Neither is a prerequisite for a same-domain pure basic query.
5. Add D8 behavior semantics and then the corresponding refinement theorem
   or explicitly bounded differential evidence. Each new mechanism profile
   requires its own admission and reproducibility gate.

The design audit is closed when its decisions, owners and failure gates are
explicit and related RFCs agree. Full engine implementation closure additionally
requires the applicable new gates above and exact-head receipts. The present
source audit closes the design choices only; publication, authenticated
coverage/conversion and general behavior refinement remain implementation work.

* Delivered: finite-family Runtime C/Python bridge (2026-10-04)

=src/ffi/temporal-family.ss= projects bounded JSON onto the existing POO
constructors and =agentic-ai-lab-temporal-model-classify=. The generic C ABI now
exposes =temporal.family.classify= and =temporal.family.observe=. Python adds
sync/async helpers without implementing classification. Model identity binds
canonical observations, provenance, family mode and completeness; execution
binding additionally binds query identity, target and budget. Observation
replays the current native model and checks every result field, including both
identities and the false authenticity/authorization fields.

The wire profile admits at most 32 domains, 128 observations, 128 hypotheses
and 256 constraints in total. It supports observed/declared/missing observations
and before/not-after constraints, with exact field sets. Unsupported syntax,
empty families, duplicate identities and zero exploration limits are rejected.
Missing cause/effect evidence remains unknown under the existing core contract.
No behavior-space interpretation is admitted by this entrypoint.

The installed macOS ARM64 CPython 3.13 wheel passed 60 tests, including 27 new
finite-family cases and the maintained lens/graph/model-tool tests. It reuses
two independent vocabularies through the same native API; tests include bounded
local refutation, overlapping families, uncertainty, permutation, query budget,
stale results and forged authority. The CI wheel gate includes the new tests.
The source/artifact qualification receipt (qualification output is an external artifact) records its exact scope.
This closes the D1 runtime connection for the finite profile and adds D4 result
fidelity evidence. It does not close source verification, derived-conclusion
admission, D7 publication or D8 behavior refinement. No real-model calls were
made in this slice; earlier lens model receipts cannot qualify this new profile.

* Delivered: host-pinned finite-family admission (2026-10-04)

=admission/interface.ss= adds native POO source-snapshot and family-admission
contracts, exported by the Temporal core interface. The admission function
replays source/model evidence, classifies the query and generates an immutable
root conclusion. Its proof identity binds source scope, evidence, assumptions,
query and explored frontier. Revision identity binds request identity and proof;
the same request under changed proof cannot reuse the earlier revision identity.

=agentic_ai_lab_semantic_source_register= is a distinct C host control entrypoint.
The generic semantic operation router rejects the reserved host prefix before
Scheme dispatch, and Python registration uses the dedicated C function on the
existing owner thread. Registration canonicalizes the domain/observation
inventory and binds source/authority/subject/scope/cut/projection/policy/generation.
The registry holds at most 128 source identities; generation rollback and
same-generation conflicting bytes are rejected. A higher generation retires
old snapshot applicability without mutating historical result values.

=temporal.family.admit= selects the current registered source by identity and
digest, replays the requested model and emits a native conclusion only for a
resolved finite-family classification. No external opaque proof ID is accepted.
Unknown classification, changed observations/provenance/clock role, missing
source or stale digest fail admission. Changing hypotheses or query budget
changes the generated proof; completeness remains a declared family assumption.

This slice establishes result/proof fidelity relative to a host-pinned source.
It does not authenticate an external issuer or prove partition/sequence
coverage. Source trust is =host-registered-snapshot=, the registry is volatile,
and receipts keep =sourceAuthenticated= and =actionAuthorized= false. No active
pointer, correction/retraction admission, authorized commit, crash recovery or
external effect is implemented by these entrypoints. The D2/D4/D6 obligations
outside this local trust premise and the D7 transaction gate remain open.

The initial ABI test exposed slot self-reference from constructor variables
named like their POO slots; renaming the value variables removed that recursion.
The regression gate covers registration, native result generation and proof
identity changes. The final installed wheel passed 78 tests, including 18 new admission cases,
and the CI wheel gate now includes that file. See the source/artifact acceptance receipt (qualification output is an external artifact) for final qualification
and the recorded initial failure. No real-model evaluation is claimed here.

* Delivered: durable authorized first-root selection (2026-10-04)

Runtime C =temporal_store= implements a local SQLite WAL/FULL transaction port;
Python =TemporalPublicationStore= uses its typed CFFI ABI. Native POO admission
and C submission share the semantic owner queue; Python performs no classification.
The durable source generation/digest and scoped host authorization policy/fence
are rechecked at the same transaction point as empty-pointer CAS, version increment
and immutable receipt insertion. Failed receipt writes roll back the pointer.
Competing connections admit at most one initial root. Identical committed retries
recover the original receipt; later source/grant retirement cannot erase it.

This closes a concrete D7 first-root local selection slice. The host explicitly
authorizes local selection; this is not Cedar or signed external permit verification.
It does not extend the existing correction/retraction POO prepare protocol or claim
its generic refinement. Initial =assert= selection requires version zero and an
empty predecessor. Business effects, correction/retraction, externally authenticated
source/coverage, general lifecycle refinement and D8 remain open. The crash gate
sends SIGKILL after commit and opens a new store to recover pointer and receipt;
it does not qualify power loss or storage hardware. See
the exact installed-wheel receipt (qualification output is an external artifact). No real-model calls were made in this slice.

Feature =mrr-transport= fixes the original MRR facade at
