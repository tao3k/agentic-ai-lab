* Scope and coupling to Slices 41, 43 and 45

[[file:../../org-elements-gql-context-design.org][Org Elements, ISO GQL, ASCENT Progressive Disclosure, and Two-Stage AST Repair]]
introduces two high-leverage mechanisms for running bounded Flash models over
rich ORG Context documents without token bloat:

1. *ASCENT Progressive Disclosure (=L0..L3=) & Dynamic Facets*: evaluates
   deductive rules at zero LLM token cost to project each ORG element at minimal
   depth (=L0= skeleton, =L1= properties + checklists, =L2= =#+RESULTS:= with
   =#+BEGIN_THINKING= folded, =L3= full raw span on active defeaters or explicit
   drill-downs).
2. *Two-Stage ORG AST Repair*: runs deterministic zero-token Stage-1 AST
   auto-repair (~90% of malformed drawer/block/cookie edits) before emitting a
   bounded diagnostic Stage-2 Flash repair request (~10%).

Without formal governance coupling to [[file:41-epistemic-consolidation-governance.org][Slice 41]] (non-droppable defeater kernel)
and [[file:45-org-sexpr-gql-candidate-exchange-search.org][Slice 45]] (structural Orgize AST admission), folding =L0..L2= could hide an
active defeater inside a collapsed subtree, or an unparsed Stage-2 Flash patch
could overwrite an advanced source cut. This slice closes both seams under
=FormalVerificationTaxonomy=. No Scheme sources are modified.
