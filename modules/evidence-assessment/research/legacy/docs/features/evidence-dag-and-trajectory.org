* Two complementary concepts

| Concept | Meaning | Primary responsibility |
|---------+---------+------------------------|
| Evidence | A fact, checked condition or completed derivation with its source/support identity and applicable validation boundary | Supply reusable support |
| Evidence DAG | Dependencies between source relations and atomic derivation components | Locate support, preserve dependency order and admit applicable reuse |
| Trajectory | An ordered record of requests, observations, evidence use, verification, failures and repairs across rounds | Explain the process and account for its cost |

A later trajectory can use applicable evidence from an earlier trajectory.
The reused object is the supported fact or derivation component. The entire
past trajectory is not automatically admitted as evidence. Evidence reuse must
remain bound to current sources, support and the relevant checker contract.

* Implemented DAG representation

=program/evidence-dag.ss= replaces =program/evidence-plan.ss=. The exported
Scheme entry is =ascent-evidence-dag= and the public engine operation is
=evidence-dag=. The build specification, dispatcher, Python transport and
qualification tests use the new name directly. Historical build receipts keep
their original source names and hashes.

Relations can contain recursive dependencies. Tarjan SCC condensation groups
recursive relations and coupled multihead relations into atomic components.
The component graph is acyclic; recursive work remains inside a component.
The =graph= action exposes:

- =nodes=: source nodes followed by dependency-first component nodes. Component
  nodes carry their relation names and whether they are recursive.
- =edges=: unique directed =from= / =to= dependencies. An edge points from
  prerequisite support to its dependent component.
- =schema=: =ascent.evidence-dag-structure.v1=.

Node IDs identify graph structure. They are not authority or completion tokens.
The structural graph does not certify source facts or prove a component's
result. Query admission retains its source identity, canonical-rule dependency
fingerprints and completed-component alias checks. Native evaluation and the
existing evidence/provenance mechanisms establish their respective result and
support boundaries. This component view does not replace fact-level provenance.

The =components=, =canonical-rule=, =query= and =slice= actions retain their
existing responsibilities under the new operation. Python's =evidence_dag=
helper transports the structural =graph= response; SCC discovery and graph
construction remain in Scheme.

* Trajectory design contract

[[file:interactive-evidence-reasoning.org][Interactive Evidence Reasoning]] specifies the intended model integration:
the model calls Scheme during candidate-path exploration and continues with
checked local support. Closed-query execution is a separate capability; it
does not qualify this interaction or prove reduced model reasoning cost.

Trajectory is the temporal view of the same work. Its event references should
connect the existing study call records to evidence feedback, using:

- Task identity, round/call identity and source identity/generation.
- The native path/result reference and applicable component fingerprints.
- Observed fresh/reused components and verifier results.
- A failed attempt, repair or invalidation event, with its reason.
- Provider-reported usage and elapsed time for each actual call.
- The first independently verified correct answer and stopping/censoring state.

Cumulative thinking before the first correct answer includes unsuccessful
attempts and repairs. Post-success work is reported separately. A lower number
of output bytes is not itself a reduction in repeated reasoning. An experiment
must distinguish a first-run derivation from a later verified reuse and from
an actual model call avoided by following an admitted result path.

A trajectory records exposed observations. It cannot reconstruct or certify
unexposed model reasoning. Failed attempts may motivate a later repair but do
not supply positive proof. Source replacement invalidates applicable result
references and reuse certificates; historical events remain historical records.

The [[file:trajectory.org][native Trajectory implementation]] now connects this contract to EvidenceChain
feedback and actual provider/grader observations. Scheme owns state transitions,
reference invalidation and prefix accounting; existing study machinery retains
raw provider request/response records. The combined qualification reports its
ABI/API, formal and experimental evidence separately.

* Local qualification

[[file:../../.cache/ascent/archived-runs/docs/features/evidence-dag-qualification.json][The qualification receipt]] records exact source hashes and local test results.
Eight direct Scheme cases check explicit SCC condensation, topological edge
ordering, deterministic graph content under declaration/rule permutation,
edge deduplication, undeclared dependencies, source-bound query reuse and source identity checks before retained-path selection,
dependency slicing, undefined derivations and atomic multihead components.
Two Python unit cases check direct operation routing and surface normalization.

The compiled engine integration test has been renamed and extended for graph
output. Its compiled ABI and API execution passed nine cases in each mode against the
new production artifact, recorded by [[file:../../.cache/ascent/archived-runs/docs/features/trajectory-qualification.json][the combined qualification receipt]].
No model efficacy, Lean refinement or Quint correspondence result is claimed
by this naming and structural-graph change.

The subsequent [[file:../research/correction-holdout/report.org][whole-task correction study]] separately qualifies source-bound
intermediate projections, task/subgoal verification scope and real failed-prefix
continuations. Its first-correct endpoint includes every preceding model call,
including the initial response and failed repairs. It reports successful and
censored trajectories together; available DAG evidence does not by itself
establish model correctness or a general reduction in thinking cost.
