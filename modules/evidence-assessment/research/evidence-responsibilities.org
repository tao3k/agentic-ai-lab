* Terms and responsibilities

| Concept | Responsibility | Outside its authority |
|---------+----------------+-----------------------|
| Evidence Chain | Track the sources, premises, and derivations supporting a local conclusion; determine current reuse and invalidation | Establish that the model understood the evidence or solved the original problem |
| Evidence DAG | Represent dependencies, sharing, and composition among premises and derivation fragments | Represent exploration order or all hidden reasoning |
| Trajectory | Record candidate branches, tool interactions, additional evidence, corrections, and costs at each round | Treat the appearance of a conclusion as proof that it holds |

An Evidence Chain need not be linear. A conclusion can have multiple premises
and support paths, represented by a DAG. Recursive structural dependencies are
first condensed into components; the structural graph is not a fact-level proof.
The current EvidenceChain interface manages sources, completed-component
credentials, and result references. Its name does not imply proof of arbitrary
natural-language derivations.

* Interaction responsibilities

The model proposes candidate branches. Scheme performs the local checks
configured by the application. Evidence checks return supported, contradicted,
or unknown under the current sources and checking conditions. The trajectory
records requests and feedback and links to evidence by reference, without
implementing another truth evaluator.

The model can declare several local premises for a candidate path; Scheme checks
their conjunction. The model remains responsible for choosing the premises.
Checking every declared premise does not repair an omitted necessary premise.
Satisfaction of the model's declared conditions is separate from completeness
and correctness of the original problem interpretation.

Supported means only that the local check succeeded; it does not certify the
whole answer. Contradicted requires explicit conflict evidence or a completed
negative check over a finite, complete domain. No match, unfinished search,
timeout, or a previous model failure must not be promoted to contradiction.

A model's claim that it used evidence can be recorded as observable behavior.
Returning evidence does not establish that its use was observed. An independent
scorer determines whole-task correctness. Actual provider usage determines
thinking cost; final-output length and trajectory text length are not substitutes.

* Model and implementation boundaries

- Scheme executes local predicates, checks source applicability, reuse, and invalidation, and maintains trajectory events and billing prefixes.
- Lean models evidence and observations as distinct types; it proves that unknown does not prune, stale evidence cannot support current judgments, and observations cannot manufacture evidence.
- Quint tracks current support, completeness, source generations, trajectory events, and costs separately; it checks false negation, stale reuse, and observations masquerading as evidence.
- Python pairs tools, transports arguments, and manages provider sessions; it neither computes relational closure nor registers model output as verified facts.

Source changes revoke affected evidence. Historical trajectories still describe
what happened at the time; they cannot be reused as current evidence.
Understanding paraphrases remains a model capability. Local predicates and entity
bindings can still misinterpret intent and require independent intent and outcome
evaluation.
