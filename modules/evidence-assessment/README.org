#+TITLE: Evidence Assessment

* Boundary

Evidence assessment compares competing hypotheses against source-labelled POO
claims and caller-declared support paths. A caller provides claims, steps,
hypotheses, and one immutable Scheme GQL Query. The module selects claim nodes
through the supported single-node GQL slice and uses Ascent to derive
source-linked steps, reachability, missing claims, and equality conflicts.
An optional POO evidence reference carries a source URI, an independence
group, and an optional content digest. Missing digests remain explicit. A
URI, source label, or digest string does not authenticate an artifact.

No domain incident, intrusion route, or policy decision is built into this
module. A result states that supplied claims support a candidate under the
declared steps. It does not authenticate a source, infer a historical
attribution, authorize an action, or assign a calibrated probability.

* Public values

[[file:objects.ss][objects.ss]] exports =PooFlowEvidenceAssessmentCase.= and the
=poo-flow-evidence-reference=, =poo-flow-evidence-claim=,
=poo-flow-evidence-step=, =poo-flow-evidence-hypothesis=, and
=poo-flow-evidence-branch= constructors. A branch proposes claims, withholds
contested claims, or does both. An inference step has either =presence= or
=equal-value= join semantics. A hypothesis names its outcome, candidate
origin, and required claims. The first value is an observed outcome; the
second is an explanatory candidate.

[[file:funs.ss][funs.ss]] exports =poo-flow-evidence-assessment-evaluate=,
=poo-flow-evidence-hypothesis-result=, and
=poo-flow-evidence-assessment-explore=. Each hypothesis result is one of
=supported=, =needs-evidence=, or =conflicted=. Missing claims preserve open
questions for every hypothesis. Equality mismatches and conflicting values
are attached to the affected hypotheses; an unrelated hypothesis retains
its own status. Each reachable hypothesis carries one bounded path of
supported step rows. =poo-flow-evidence-assessment-explore= evaluates up to
eight caller-authored branches against every hypothesis, preserving the
baseline, changed statuses, witness paths, and remaining gaps for each
branch. It emits no preferred branch, probability, or next action.

The Case is declarative. The private GQL and Ascent tuples are projections
of its POO values. The consumer must keep a source-authenticity and Host
admission boundary outside this engine. Branches grant no tool execution.
Downstream investigators must not count claims in one independence group as
independent corroboration.

* Qualification

Run =just test-file t/evidence-assessment-core-test.ss=. The focused test checks
GQL selection, two competing hypotheses, a matching pair, a conflicting
source that cannot be hidden by a matching pair, and rejection of a negative
claim posed as positive evidence. A cyclic candidate graph reaches a finite
fixed point under Ascent's explicit input, derivation, and output bounds. The
test also checks a concrete witness path, three opposing hypothetical
branches, unbound content digests, and rejection of a source-impersonating
reference.
