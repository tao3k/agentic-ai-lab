Quint source authority and commands are documented in [[file:README.org][the proof inventory]].
=TemporalFamily.qnt= separates supplied observations, constraints, exclusive
family completeness and exploration limit. All enabled candidate orders are
checked. A bounded frontier cannot establish necessity; its mutant must fail
=TerminalAgreement=. Temporal admissibility alone does not establish causation.

=TemporalRevision.qnt= preserves predecessor admission, concurrent-head
uncertainty and the historical admission/valid-time cut. No implicit winner
is selected between r2 and r3.

=TemporalInvalidation.qnt= computes the least reverse-dependency closure by a
finite fold bounded by conclusion count. =advance= chooses any pending member.
Scope reprojection schedules all affected conclusions. Its mutation must fail
=DirectScheduled=. =TemporalConclusionSelection.qnt= assumes an atomic CAS,
checks one committed proposal and rejects the stale competing proposal. Its
mutation must fail =AtMostOneCommit=.

Weak-fairness obligations retain the finite terminal reachability assumptions.
They do not promise termination under an unfair runtime scheduler. Stuttering
is implicit in the verifier's temporal specification. Public POO values and
functional APIs are independent of these internal action relations. There is
no general implementation refinement theorem across Scheme, Rust and Quint.
