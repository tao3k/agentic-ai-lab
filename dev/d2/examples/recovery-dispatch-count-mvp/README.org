The first PNG showed the three evidence conditions converging on the
same question. Reading it revealed that opposing signs beside R43 and
R44 could look like arithmetic cancellation. The revised artwork names
the two observations and labels their evidence directly: one was a copy,
the other a distinct replay. The review preserves the first finding.

The revised PNG was viewed again. Its labels distinguish the two changes
without arithmetic symbols. All three relations converge on the same
question. ASCENT grounded 11 anchors across the four facets in the
[[file:../../output/recovery-dispatch-count-mvp/review.json][final evaluation]].
