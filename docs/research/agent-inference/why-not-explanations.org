* Assessment for gerbil-ascent

Returning a row is not an explanation.  A why answer needs a valid rule
instance and supporting source rows.  A why-not answer must declare
its domain, which missing premise or failed rule is being discussed,
and whether the search was complete.  For recursive rules, a single
finite witness can be useful but does not claim all provenance; the
PUG theorem is not a recursive provenance theorem for this Library.
Canonical semantic lineage and fact admission remain with MRR.

* Experiment

On a finite diamond graph with two paths to one goal, demand one
witness and then all bounded alternatives; remove one edge and retain
the surviving path.  For an absent goal, require a domain-bounded gap
and distinguish =no derivation under complete snapshot= from
=not evaluated/partial=.  Compare every explanation with the actual
rule/source graph before sending it to an Agent.
