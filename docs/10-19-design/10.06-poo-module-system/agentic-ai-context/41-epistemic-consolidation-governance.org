* Scope and relationship to Slice 40

Where [[file:40-context-governance-boundary.org][Slice 40]] specifies the outer Context-Governance boundary, trust-role
lattice, restriction meet, and threat-gated Provider handoff, this slice governs
the *epistemic interior* of the Context System across two transitions:

1. *Gate 2 (Bounded Epistemic Projection and Compaction)*: projecting an inquiry
   with competing hypotheses, active defeaters and mandatory restrictions into a
   finite token or row budget without stripping counterevidence or manufacturing
   false certainty ([[file:workspace-recovery.org][workspace recovery inquiry contract]]).
2. *Gate 3 (Short-to-Long Memory Consolidation and Recall)*: promoting
   short-term observations into versioned long-term knowledge under
   outcome-anchored evidence, three-way Separation of Duties, retraction
   propagation, and explicit ORG source-promotion grants
   ([[file:01-system-boundary.org][Memory lifecycle boundary]] and [[file:org-durable-declarations.org][Org durable intent]]).

No Scheme source files are added or modified by this design slice.

* Gate 2: Non-droppable kernel and =OmittedAlternativesReceipt=

An inquiry $q$ at evidence cut $E_\sigma$ exposes competing hypotheses
$\mathcal{H}$, supporting premises, active defeaters $\mathrm{Defeat}(h)$, open
questions, and mandatory restriction metadata $R$. Under a finite projection
budget $B \in \mathbb{N}$, compaction is governed by two strict invariants:

| Governance rule | Required projection behavior | Prevented failure mode |
|-----------------+------------------------------+------------------------|
| Non-droppable governance kernel | Every active defeater $d \in \mathrm{Defeat}(h)$ of any retained hypothesis $h$, together with mandatory restrictions $R$, must be retained in full; if $\mathrm{Cost}(R) + \mathrm{Cost}(S) + \mathrm{Cost}(\mathrm{Defeat}(S)) > B$, the projection fails closed (=refusedOverBudget=) | Stripping caveats, restrictions or counterevidence to fit a prompt window (=T-CTX-03=) |
| Explicit omission receipt for competing hypotheses | Selecting a proper subset $S \subsetneq \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{live}}$ of undefeated hypotheses is permitted under budget $B$ only when the projection attaches an =OmittedAlternativesReceipt= recording the omitted hypothesis identities and sets =claimsExhaustive = false= | Collapsing unresolved competing hypotheses into an apparently unique conclusion |

#+begin_src typst
$ upright("ProjectGoverned")(R, cal(H), S, B) = cases(
  upright("RefusedOverBudget") & "if" upright("Cost")(R) + upright("Cost")(S) + |upright("Defeat")(S)| > B,
  upright("Admitted")(S, upright("Defeat")(S), upright("none"), upright("exhaustive"): #t) & "if within budget and" cal(H)_(upright("live")) backslash S = emptyset,
  upright("Admitted")(S, upright("Defeat")(S), upright("some")(cal(H)_(upright("live")) backslash S), upright("exhaustive"): #f) & "otherwise"
) $
#+end_src

Downstream reasoning and governance rules must reject any rule expecting an
exhaustive single-cause attribution whenever =claimsExhaustive = false= or an
=OmittedAlternativesReceipt= is present.

* Gate 3: Anti-poisoning consolidation, three-way SoD and ORG promotion

Long-term retention in =Context::Memory= is neither text aging, read-count
accumulation, nor an irreversible truth bit. Proposing a reusable long-term
abstraction $m$ from short-term experience requires four conjoint conditions:

1. *Authenticated outcome anchoring*: $m$ binds at least one authenticated,
   currently supported =ActualOutcomeReceipt= issued by the execution/evidence
   owner. Model confidence, turn completion or summary compactness alone cannot
   satisfy this premise.
2. *Explicit scope and defeat conditions*: $m$ records both its applicability
   assumptions and explicit defeat conditions under which future observations
   force reassessment.
3. *Three-way Separation of Duties (=ThreeWaySoD=)*: the proposing Agent/Model
   (=proposer=), the outcome-issuing evidence owner (=evidenceOwner=), and the
   consolidation reviewer/policy authority (=approver=) must be pairwise
   distinct:

#+begin_src typst
$ upright("ThreeWaySoD")(p, e, a) equiv (p != e) and (p != a) and (e != a) $
#+end_src

4. *Revalidated recall and explicit ORG promotion*:
   - Recalling $m$ into a later Session or Worktree creates a candidate slice
     that requires the *consumer's own active read grant* (origin grants never
     transfer across consumers) and live support for $m$'s bound outcome.
     Retracting or invalidating that outcome withdraws current recall while
     preserving $m$ in historical audit lineage.
   - Consolidating $m$ inside Memory never edits an ORG document. Promoting an
     insight into an ORG source requires a separate expected-revision write
     grant and triggers a fresh Orgize parse and Query generation.
