* Decision

The research unit is an admitted fact, determined condition, or checked
derivation fragment. Different questions can reuse the same unit without
having the same subproblem or final answer. Search, optimization and divergent
exploration are primary workloads; context compression is an additional stressor.

Test two outcomes independently: lower cumulative effort at comparable success,
and better solution quality at the same total resource budget. A cache may
achieve one without the other. The proposed distinction is compositional,
source-bound logical reuse with explicit withdrawal, not simply persistent
memory or executable checking, which have direct prior art.

This note extends [[file:verified-reasoning-cache.org][the initial study]]. The
machine-readable [[file:../../../.cache/ascent/archived-runs/docs/research/agent-inference/factor-reuse-pilot-plan.json][pilot plan]] is a design
artifact, not an executed or preregistered experiment. Exact model, task
manifests, budgets and grader versions must be frozen before execution.

The sections below this update preserve the October 10 reading and historical

fragment reuse. No additional model experiment was executed for this update;
the proposed diagnostic below is not frozen or an efficacy result. Historical
tasks, judges, annotations and failed receipts remain unchanged.

The subject is external assistance to a frozen model's trained reasoning:
ASCENT evaluates admitted relations and supplies checked computational
consequences. It does not own the complete search, model training, a serving
cache or Agentic AI Lab's authority and Temporal Causality. Lean specifications and
Quint protocol models constrain particular Library behaviors; they do not
prove that an LLM will use a supplied premise or stop deriving it again.

** Small mechanism diagnostic before another optimization claim

Use a finite admitted program with one shared positive premise P and different
residual premises L and R. For example, P is =path(0,2)=; a left obligation
needs P and =path(3,4)=, while a right obligation needs P and =path(5,6)=.
The downstream obligations differ even though their ground premise is shared.
Keep both obligations within one admitted program/evidence scope; do not
pretend a certificate for a restricted query covers another program.

The consumer presents a fixed pair of sibling obligations in the same order
to all three arms, then a novel residual obligation. This schedule ensures
an opportunity for cross-path reuse without an instruction to avoid thinking.
It is a proposed Library diagnostic, not a replacement that makes the failed
optimization task pass. Publish its exact data, ordinary questions, schedule,
judge and annotation rules before any model call. The prior tasks stay frozen.

| Arm | Evidence access | Common controls |
|-----+-----------------+-----------------|
| baseline | Raw source and existing native query interface | Same obligations, action schema, context policy and total budget |
| Fact | Same access plus source-bound outcomes for the relevant atoms | Outcomes selected by obligation syntax, not by a hidden optimal answer |
| Scheme | The same Fact outcomes plus admitted ancestor-closed fragments | Same available siblings; disable pruning in this fragment diagnostic so pruning is not a second intervention |

Construction, lookup, validation and rendering costs remain recorded. Facts
and fragments are explicit inputs, never free model knowledge. Build them
from the supplied source with the Library; the independent judge remains a
separate finite graph/rule oracle. Validate it against missing-rule and
wrong-join mutations and compare intermediate atoms as well as final answers.
Retain schema violations, unknown evidence, stale handles and failed calls.

Freeze repetition categories before running: (1) supplied premise consumed,
(2) positive premise independently rederived, (3) completed-scope absence
rechecked, (4) new residual inference, (5) resource/objective arithmetic and
(6) ambiguous or unsupported span. A reference to P alone is not rederivation;
require a literal span containing an actual supporting argument. Repeated
arithmetic is reported separately, not relabeled as a cached Datalog proof.
Blindly adjudicate sampled baseline/treatment spans with a second annotator
and publish disagreements and rejected labels. An unvalidated zero is unknown.

Test source change separately: remove a support for P, try the old handle,
resolve against the new source, and independently grade the changed answers.
Also retain an unchanged-source control and a change with alternative positive
support. Do not convert failure to find a bounded witness into finite absence.

Three claims have separate admission conditions:

1. *Correct reuse*: shared support is reused and all relevant answers agree
   with the independent oracle, including stale-source controls.
2. *Reduced repetition*: baseline visibly rederives the shared premise and
   the evidence arms reduce that signal with comparable correctness and
   validated annotation coverage. Native hits alone do not satisfy this gate.
3. *Productive reallocation*: only after the mechanism gate, an independently
   frozen harder fixture permits new valid consequences or complete solutions
   within the same input-plus-all-output budget. Prefix length alone does not
   establish deeper reasoning or higher quality.

Charge cached/replayed input and all output including thinking and failures.
Report proof construction and native work separately, along with annotation
cost. Reservation stops censor outcomes; unused budget is not savings. Model
aliases, repeated trials and context policies must be disclosed before causal
claims. This update changes research interpretation and the proposed gate;
it adds no Library framework, prompt intervention or model-results claim.

* Six primary method readings (historical 2026-10-10)

Full-text HTML methods, evaluation and limitations were inspected on October
10, 2026. Source versions are explicit. Percentages are author-reported; no
paper reproduction or artifact execution is claimed. The word "verified"
has different operational meanings across these papers.

** ENGRAM-R: direct fact reuse and a critical matched ablation

[[https://arxiv.org/html/2511.12987v3][Reuse, Don't Recompute]], v3 March 2, 2026
(first submitted November 17, 2025): sections 3--4, 6 and Appendix B.
Typed retrieval produces cards containing an identifier, claim and anchor;
the model cites identifiers. The answering model is frozen; a separate model
maintains memory. Appendix B compares cards plus citation control against the
same retrieval/store/backbone and record budget. Its overall reasoning-token
reduction is 47.8%, distinct from the abstract's 75% against full context.
Open-domain judge accuracy falls from 71.5 to 54.2 despite shorter reasoning.
This supports a matched card-rendering baseline and forbids treating brevity
as improvement. Anchors and citations provide traceability, not an ASCENT
derivation certificate. The limitations explicitly identify stale cards.
The reported artifact locator is an anonymous repository; availability and
reproduction have not been audited here.

** AtomMem: atomic storage is relevant, but extraction remains a model task

[[https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19847v1][AtomMem]], v1 June 18, 2026:
sections 3.1--3.5 and limitations. An SFT-trained extractor produces standalone
facts with participant, keyword, time and event metadata. Section 3.1.3
retrieves potentially related facts and uses an LLM to generate nonredundant
content and updates for duplicates/conflicts. Its associative graph organizes
recall. This is a natural fact-granularity comparator. That verification is
not independent finite rule replay: extractors and update decisions can err.
Our primary study freezes the answering model and reports any extractor
training/build costs separately. Use reviewed extraction only in a diagnostic
condition, then test actual extraction separately; do not silently supply an
oracle fact bank to the proposed method alone.

** ReFact: supporting evidence can be local to one inference step

[[https://arxiv.org/html/2607.20833v1][ReFact]], v1 July 23, 2026;
the metadata lists v2 July 29, but this method reading pins v1:
sections 3 and 4 plus evaluation tables. A teacher constructs citation-grounded
traces; SFT followed by RL teaches when to restate source evidence and at what
granularity. Restatements can be smaller than sentences. This motivates a
packet that exposes only the factor needed for a local inference. The approach
changes model parameters and does not by itself establish persistent,
dependency-valid cross-task reuse. It belongs in the training-assisted secondary
comparison, not as a supposedly identical frozen-backbone control. Source
attribution, answer sufficiency and derivation correctness are separate checks.

** CounterMem: checked local alternatives are already direct prior art

[[https://arxiv.org/html/2609.31874v1][CounterMem]], v1 September 25, 2026:
sections 3--4, Appendix A and metric conventions. A failed action is followed by
local alternatives evaluated from reset/copied state with tests, proof checkers
or solvers. Improved outcomes, including partial improvements, can become
records with original/corrected actions, evidence and applicability conditions.
A trained selector chooses one record or skips; memory and policy freeze for
held-out tasks. Appendix A says the selector cannot directly compose multiple
records and records can become stale. Reported task-run token savings exclude
offline selector training. This motivates checked correction reuse as a
comparator and requires lifecycle accounting. Checked local improvement is
not universal correctness. Code is promised upon acceptance; the metadata's
"ICLR 2027" comment is not evidence of acceptance or a reproducible release.

** SEMV: scoped revision and memory transfer need separate measurements

[[https://arxiv.org/html/2609.27175v1][Self-Evolving Multimedia Verification]],
v1 September 23, 2026: sections 4.5--4.6. It retains provenance-bearing
observations, failure lessons and semantic rules. Memory has scope, supporting
cases/source fingerprints, conflict and lifecycle information. Its verification
gate combines grounding identifiers, confidence and a safety check; test cases
cannot seed activated memory. Scoped revision uses dependency-aware recomputation.
These are close lifecycle ideas, but argument confidence is not the same object
as a replayable finite relational proof. Adopt source-fingerprint deduplication,
negative-transfer measurement and evaluation isolation; investigate the actual
checker and native representation before claiming equivalence or superiority.

** Stale constraints: confirmed-looking factors can misdirect exploration

[[https://arxiv.org/html/2608.25553v3][When Stale Constraints Go Unchecked]],
v3 August 28, 2026: methods, sections 4 and 6. A scripted six-memory scenario
limits source inspection to two records. A superseded constraint is often
followed unless the relevant source path is inspected. The forced-critical
intervention uses experimenter knowledge and is an oracle positive control,
not a general routing algorithm. The target-blind constraint-priority control
is separately evaluated. Its scripted store does not estimate deployment
staleness prevalence. Use unchanged-source and stale-source controls, hide
the critical path from routing, and measure false reuse as well as missed reuse.
A provenance link alone does not ensure that an agent validates it.

* Historical pilot proposal: four arms and two comparisons

| Arm | Memory / checking behavior | What it isolates |
|-----+----------------------------+------------------|
| A: fresh | Raw source tools and fresh ASCENT checks | Existing external assistance without retention |
| B: cards | Persistent fact cards with stable IDs/anchors, same evidence access | Representation and ordinary fact memory |
| C: checked factors | Current-source checked atomic consequences, with attached scope | Fact validation/retention without supplied derivation fragments |
| D: checked fragments | C plus composable checked derivation segments and support dependencies | Incremental value of derivation reuse |

All arms can access the same source and checker. A can derive any provided
consequence, but spends its own matched budget doing so. C/D obtain records
through measured construction, not hidden answers. B gets the same extracted
candidate claims and comparable retrieval/rendering budgets, but does not gain
an independent validity gate for free. Static-source mechanism diagnostics can
share a reviewed extraction manifest across all arms; results from this oracle
diagnostic must be reported separately from end-to-end extraction/retrieval.

Use the same concise-answer and record-citation instruction where applicable.
Do not tell only D to avoid repeated thinking. Compare identical packets with
different gates in a separate validity ablation; counterbalance record order
and neutral labels to control authority-language and verbosity effects.

First comparison: fixed resource caps, acceptance and solution quality; then
cost to independently accepted result. Second: exactly matched resource caps
with progressively harder residual tasks; test if C/D achieve higher quality
or more independently accepted new consequences. Do not turn token savings
into fictitious extra budget. The model must actually perform productive new
work within the shared cap. Early completion remains an outcome, not a reason
to force unnecessary reasoning.

** Workload families and factor examples

| Family | Shared determined component | Different residual questions | Independent outcome |
|--------+-----------------------------+------------------------------+---------------------|
| Dependency/search planning | Source-bound reachability, permitted transition, affected-set membership | Candidate ordering, admissible plan, minimum cost among valid plans | Finite enumerator / exhaustive small-instance optimum |
| Constraint composition | Checked membership, local feasibility, scoped blocker | Different combinations/branches with shared and novel constraints | Finite solver with withheld counterexamples |
| Optimization exploration | Verified invariance/compatibility facts plus separately measured local performance | Different plans, new bottlenecks, multi-constraint tradeoffs | Correctness oracle plus actual matched performance measurements |
| Repository repair | Checked dependency or domain-condition factor from live source | Distinct edits requiring additional reasoning | Hidden regression tests and passing controls |

Optimization measurements are environment-bound observations; they cannot be
used as sound pruning rules without an applicable bound. For a small finite
plan corpus, graph dependencies may be evaluated by ASCENT while an independent
enumerator grades global quality. Real repository tasks require a broader
grader; the existing two-task pilot remains a pipeline diagnostic.

Each sequence begins with charged construction and asks distinct residual
questions sharing selected factors. Include zero reuse, partial reuse and high
reuse. Vary the unresolved tail difficulty while keeping the shared factors
fixed. Include alternatives that look alike but have different typed meanings.
Then introduce irrelevant edits, one lost support with an alternative surviving,
loss of the last support, a new negative blocker and changed aggregate inputs.
Finally add controlled compaction. Primary tests do not require compaction.

** Budget, metrics and causal limits

The design pilot uses 4 families x 2 independent instances x 4 arms x 2 total
generated-token budgets x 2 repetitions = 128 sequence trajectories, before
secondary ablations. This estimates variance and pipeline feasibility; eight
instances are not a paper-scale population. Candidate caps are 8,192 and 16,384
generated tokens including reported reasoning, 8 turns and 65,536 cumulative
input tokens per trajectory. Resolve provider support before freezing them.
All extractor/controller model calls count against the arm's total cap.
Checker work, retrieval and memory construction are separately capped and
charged; an equal-dollar lifecycle comparison is required as a robustness test.

Provider-reported reasoning-token totals measure aggregate effort. A structured
external action journal records source generation, factor ID, prior validity,
check request, cache decision, visible supporting IDs and accepted consequence.
Repeated eligible check requests are an observable mechanism proxy. They do
not locate repetitions inside opaque thinking or attribute tokens to a Fact.
Do not require access to private reasoning text or treat citations as faithful
internal process measurements.

Report acceptance at both budget levels, objective gap for optimization,
independently checked new consequences/feasible alternatives, repeated eligible
checks, stale-use rate, false rejection and lifecycle cost. Reasoning depth is
the independently verified dependency depth of newly solved obligations, with
diagnostic shortest proof depth available; neither trace length nor cycles
count as deeper thought. More branches are useful only if they contribute
correct new solutions or improved final quality.

Compare paired differences by instance and seed; retain timeouts and censored
work. Estimate uncertainty by task clusters, not turns. The small pilot reports
descriptive intervals only and cannot prove noninferiority. Predeclare a
justified success margin and power analysis for the later confirmatory study.
Acceptance, repair and source-selection oracles remain hidden; no evaluation
answer or test trace enters persistent training memory. Within-sequence memory
is allowed and labeled separately from frozen cross-task transfer.

* Falsification and required ablations

- If B matches C/D, compact fact presentation may explain the benefit; no
  evidence for an additional verification/derivation contribution follows.
- If reasoning tokens fall but success or optimization quality falls, the
  intervention has not shown quality-controlled savings.
- If cost falls but equal-budget quality/depth does not improve, support the
  efficiency hypothesis only, not budget reallocation.
- If D matches C, proof-fragment composition adds no established benefit on
  this corpus. Charge its maintenance overhead nonetheless.
- If gains exist only with reviewed/oracle extraction or routing, do not claim
  an end-to-end agent mechanism. Run noisy extraction and non-oracle retrieval.
- If stale factors mislead the model, evaluate current-source replay, coarse
  invalidation and future selective support withdrawal independently.

After the primary pilot, use a cards/citation factorial, checked-correction
memory baseline modeled on CounterMem, a fixed selector versus learned selector
comparison, and a separate KV-on/off factorial. Keep exact-answer caching out
of the distinct-question main comparison. Paper-scale novelty and efficacy
remain conditional on these outcomes.
