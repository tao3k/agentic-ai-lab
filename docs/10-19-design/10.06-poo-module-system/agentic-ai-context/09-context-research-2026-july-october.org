* User direction and scope

The user rejects context compression as the design route. Preserve original
Context, source bytes, proof/Derivation/premise lineage, counterevidence and
history. A bounded working view selects permitted information for a question;
it does not replace the underlying Context with a lossy summary. A reusable
interpretation remains a separate versioned artifact linked to originals.
No compaction, summarizer or destructive context rewrite is added by this work.

The selected review window is 2026-07-01 through 2026-10-25, inclusive. Dates
below are first arXiv submission dates, with inspected revisions noted where
relevant. Primary abstracts were checked for all entries. CarryOn, Scroll and
ContextPipe also received targeted full-method reading. This is a selected
research map, not a claim to have found every paper or reproduced its results.
Earlier MemGPT/A-MEM/ACE comparisons remain historical background in
[[file:07-context-literature-comparison.org][07]], not the main recent-paper corpus.

* Closest comparisons and design consequences

** Scroll

Scroll separates a losslessly retained event/payload environment from the
working view, with programmatic retrieval and exposure. This directly weakens
any claim that retaining raw history outside a prompt and selecting projections
is itself a novel contribution. Our comparison must instead inspect explicit
registered inference/support applicability, restriction/consumer admission and
Attempt terminal publication under changing owners. This is a proposed axis of
comparison, not an assertion that Scroll has no safety controls.

** CarryOn

CarryOn's Method and Appendix A describe reusable object carriers, supporting
evidence, environment checks, versions and validation before commitment. We
must not present object-oriented Context, versioned records or evidence-bound
updates as our exclusive inventions. The specific question for our native work
is whether original ASCENT proof/support/journal bindings and owner-fenced
Attempt outcomes reject withdrawn evidence and late results. CarryOn's method
must be evaluated on its own semantics rather than relabeled as summarization.

** ContextPipe

ContextPipe's typed pipeline and replay traces are relevant to our separation
of Query, projection and serialization. Its Section 3.5 describes a fail-open
placeholder when spill rehydration fails. Our required-evidence profile instead
refuses or returns a declared partial result: an unavailable required source
cannot be replaced by a placeholder while claiming complete admission. That is
a concrete contract difference, not a benchmark superiority claim.

** Stale binding and evidence substitution

CICM studies selection of outdated values despite current information being
present. Same Simulation, Different Evidence studies a different failure:
answers can use substituted evidence even when the simulator computed the right
value. These motivate distinct tests for retained bytes, selected evidence,
current applicability, and actual answer grounding. Our native current-owner
gate does not itself prove that an LLM used the admitted evidence correctly.

* Advantages that require specific evidence

The candidate contribution is a composed executable contract: retain originals;
carry exact original inference and support lineage; change current applicability
on premise withdrawal without deleting history; admit each consumer under
current owners; conditionally publish one terminal result per Attempt. POO is
the native composition mechanism, Org/Query owns addressable content, ASCENT /
temporal-causality owns declared derivation/support applicability, and Session
independently owns interaction identity and lifecycle.

A local owner CAS and proof-relative native tests can support those specific
claims. They do not prove factual truth of inputs, correctness of arbitrary
inference programs, complete causal attribution, better LLM reasoning, cheaper
serving, durable multi-process recovery or priority over all related work.

* Evaluation changes implied by the no-compression route

- Measure exact original-source recovery and correct evidence binding; do not
  reward a smaller prompt when it removes a required premise or counterexample.
- Separately test source withdrawal, unsupported claims, permission withdrawal,
  foreign Session use, stale publication, cancellation and recovery obligations.
- Record task quality and refusals, whole-request latency/cost and all auxiliary
  calls, not only token count or the context manager's own runtime.
- Add retrieval-selection mistakes and evidence substitution to failure controls;
  preserving originals does not guarantee an Agent will select or use them.
- Retain negative outcomes. The forgetting paper itself reports workloads without
  savings and a lower-quality continuation, illustrating why universal savings
  cannot be inferred from one token/cost example.

No new paid model calls, training run or paper benchmark was executed. The
current work implements and tests the native owner-publication boundary; the
papers guide explicit comparisons and future matched experiments.

* Connection to the existing Context lifecycle

| Existing lifecycle step | Preserved responsibility | Research / acceptance question |
|-------------------------+--------------------------+--------------------------------|
| Information entry | Content / Org source identities and versions; declared evidence admission | Can the exact original artifact be recovered, including multimodal source? |
| Working Context formation | Query, scoped composition and admitted consumer projection | Does selected evidence retain original bindings and explicit omissions? |
| Use and result publication | Independent Session Attempt; Context native current-owner guard | Can a stale source/grant or cancelled Attempt still publish a result? |
| Reassessment | Original ASCENT proof retained; support/journal applicability recomputed | Does required-premise withdrawal remove current eligibility without erasing history? |
| Long-term knowledge integration | New scoped claim/procedure with origins, alternatives, failures and defeaters | Is generalization justified by actual outcomes rather than a compressed retelling? |
| Cross-Session recall | A new consumer request, projection, admission and grant | Is recalled knowledge reassessed rather than inheriting the old Session's authority? |
