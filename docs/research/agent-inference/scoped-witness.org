* Source and why it is core

Dezhuang Miao et al., [[https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/miao26e.html][LLM-MatLogic: Executable Exchange Contracts for
Knowledge-Graph Query Answering with Scoped Negation]], ICML 2026;
[[https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/miao26e/miao26e.pdf][paper PDF]]. It studies an AI specifier paired with a deterministic
graph executor on knowledge-graph tasks. The problem is central to
ASCENT: a model can specify the wrong graph logic even when the engine
executes that logic correctly, and a returned entity may need a
checkable witness with exclusion scope intact.

The paper's matrix executor and exchange contract are *not* Datalog
semantics. Its result still directly informs the candidate-program,
snapshot, query and evidence boundary in this repository.

* Executable transfer

The [[file:wikidata-scoped-exclusion.org][Wikidata scoped exclusion fixture]] already checks branch-scoped exclusion on pinned finite
Wikidata claims. The [[file:../../features/candidate-feedback/acceptance.org][candidate feedback contract]] now adds
=reasoning-compare-attempts=: compare submitted and gold program receipts only
after independent same-source/content binding, completion and exact full query
agreement. Frozen scope controls produce exact missing/extra rows under the
same native executor, with Lean qualification premises and Quint fault controls.
This makes gold-versus-proposal grading available without rerunning execution.
The feedback transfer now has a shared 1,024-state finite corpus: Quint's actual
transition evaluator exports it, Lean checks current/frozen results against a
kernel-checked executable grader, and Scheme replays public APIs with real
native receipts while checking the input abstraction premises. CI rejects
regeneration or native result drift. This strengthens result correspondence;
it does not change the model-experiment or arbitrary-source proof boundary.
It does not supply a model proposal, certify gold intent, implement MatLogic's
matrix schedule or reproduce its benchmark. Raw response capture, model/request
identity, retrieval provenance and the actual model study remain open.

* Code correspondence and missing boundary

The [[file:../../features/scoped-witness/acceptance.org][scoped-witness application]] now makes the section 2.1 timing example
executable: retain =(start,middle,target)=, exclude only the middle before
projection, and preserve separate direct support. Two allowed/blocked
alternatives to one answer exercise information lost by answer-only filtering.
A shared 256-input corpus checks the actual Scheme Program/Session, Quint
transition evaluator and executable Lean semantics; four named faults test

an explicit two-hop scope contract, not the full EEC or sparse-matrix schedule.

The branch-scoped extension now addresses the section 2.1 Movie-A/Movie-B
disjunction example and section 2.2's scoped exclusion requirement. Shared graph
edges join scope-tagged endpoint selections; intermediate masks retain that
scope in witness and answer rows before a separate union projection. The
shared 1,024-input BranchScopedWitness corpus tests both independent masks,
two alternative paths and each branch's direct support through Scheme, Lean
and Quint. Lean establishes locality over arbitrary scope/vertex types; the
native tests use caller-supplied symbolic identities and retained source updates.
This transfer implements two-hop disjunctive composition. The subsequent
[[file:../../features/scoped-witness/composition.org][scoped composition]] layer implements group-local OR and required-group AND
over the same endpoint pairs, following Algorithm 1 lines 19–30. Its shared
512-input corpus qualifies group membership, empty groups, candidate boundaries
and published evidence; Lean relates stratified missing-group negation to the
declarative universal/existential condition. The unified
[[file:../../features/scoped-witness/query.org][bounded scoped query]] now compiles K-hop layers, structural masks, group
composition and shortest-path traces as one Program. Its 1,536 complete
requests share Scheme execution, independent Lean grading and Quint transitions.
Pair relations do not replicate the paper's general typed evidence-space joins
or its sparse-matrix implementation.

* Concrete discriminator

Choose one frozen Freebase-derived or similarly licensed real KG
snapshot with known entity/relation mappings and independently
constructed structured queries. Include (a) a positive multi-hop
query, (b) two branches where an exclusion applies to only one branch,
(c) a witness that disappears if negation is delayed until after
projection, and (d) a source triple withdrawal. Keep retrieved-subgraph
membership separate from the complete source snapshot.

For each query, record the raw model proposal before feedback, the
admitted Scheme program, full answer relation, per-answer witness,
typed rejection/unsupported/budget status, source generation and the
gold query's result under the same finite snapshot. Score proposal
validity, exact answer sets, exclusion-scope fidelity, witness replay,
and natural-language answer separately. Compare a gold structured
request with the model-generated request under the same executor to
measure specification loss. Repeat eligible positive paths through
Set and =trrel= storage only after both are independently proved to
denote the same relation on that fixture.

The end-to-end model study remains proposed. Wikidata scoped exclusion and the shared scope/feedback
corpora already implement its finite executor controls; they do not establish
that ASCENT runs the paper's benchmark. Full KG truth, NL intent, and incomplete-subgraph
answers remain distinct from finite Datalog closure.

* Structural mask semantics and executable distinction

[[file:../../features/scoped-witness/reachability-mask.org][Scoped structural reachability masks]] transfers section 3.1's explicit
=pre(z)⊗suf(z)= formula into recursive Scheme relations and the same group
composer. Lean and Scheme check the diamond counterexample; Quint detects
clean-path substitution on the shared three-node cycle corpus, which is also
graded by Lean and replayed by Scheme. These are two concrete presentations of
the same contract distinction: structural pair exclusion and existential
clean-path survival differ. The
finite least-closure producer retains its own contract. The unified bounded
request entry additionally implements K-hop production and shortest predecessor
publication. Its revised contract bounds the *sum* of exclusion prefix and suffix
lengths by the request K. The paper's independently bounded sides remain an
explicit comparison control, rather than the default bounded query semantics.

* Proof-guided total-hop experiment

The transferable design points are scope ownership, evidence before projection,
and a unified production/mask/composition/trace schedule. We do not infer that
our executor has higher KGQA accuracy or throughput from its architecture.

For a fixed directed graph, =Via(K,s,z,t)= means there exist exact prefix/suffix
walks of lengths p and q with p+q≤K. =via_iff_shortest= proves this iff the
shortest segment lengths satisfy the same sum bound. =exact_append= proves the
segments concatenate into an actual bounded producer walk; =zero_via= rules
out removing an identity pair through an unrelated returning cycle at K=0.
These laws justify using existing shortest relations in the Scheme mask rule.

Quint independently enumerates exact segment lengths in its expected predicate,
while actual transitions and Scheme use shortest distances. All 1,536 request
observations remain linked to Lean grading, scope/group tables and final traces.
The =independentSides= mutation reinstates the paper formula and must produce a
counterexample to the total-hop invariant. On the same census, 156 observations
change. This establishes a reproducible semantic difference, not a superiority
claim or replication of the paper's natural-language benchmark.

Structural exclusion still rejects a pair when any *in-bound* banned walk
exists, even if a clean alternative exists. The least-closure producer retains
its separate unbounded structural contract. This experiment does not truncate
the general ASCENT fixed-point engine or replace the remaining typed evidence
join/interface/projection architecture work.
