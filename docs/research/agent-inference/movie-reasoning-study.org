* Research objective and corrected mechanism

The complete English report is
[[file:scheme-thinking-experiment-report.md][Scheme Evidence and Complex Model Thinking]].
It prints all four complex questions, the matched protocol, every trajectory,
first-answer accuracy, cumulative thinking, failures, repair costs and artifact
identities. The original protocol and all adaptive follow-ups are separate.

The corrected tool-history primary evaluation completed 16 trajectories:
control 7/8 verified and Scheme 8/8 verified. On the seven jointly verified
pairs, cumulative thinking decreased 60.92%. All assigned trajectories consumed
560,342 control and 199,765 Scheme thinking tokens, including censoring.
Calls increased from 21 to 33; this is a thinking reduction, not fewer rounds.
Four-hop, recursive, scoped-union and universal-family matched reductions were
78.56%, 23.63%, 60.16% (one verified pair), and 58.02% respectively. One recursive
pair increased thinking 22.55%; the aggregate does not erase that negative result.

All eight assisted trials published checked evidence, but no later query
explicitly extended a published alias. These results do not isolate incremental
evidence reuse from execution assistance. Complete provider reasoning and paired
tool results are retained in both arms. Original action-ledger trials did not
retain actual provider thinking history and are not pooled with these trials.

The model receives the complete complex question and reasons about it. Scheme
checks externally expressed facts, constraints and derivations, retains their
source-bound evidence, and returns that evidence to subsequent reasoning rounds.
The hypothesis is that this enhances subsequent thinking by avoiding repeated
inference and incorrect branches. A model-first subproblem decomposition is
not a prerequisite. Hidden model thinking is not read or certified by Scheme.
A derived relation certifies consequences of admitted rules and finite facts;
it does not certify that a model-generated rule captures natural-language intent.

There are two distinct validations:

1. Enhancement during reasoning: cumulative work from the complete question
   until the first independently verified correct answer, including failed
   attempts, scans, query synthesis, reflection, repairs and evidence construction.
2. Reuse after validation: an admitted identical task on the same source can
   follow the established path with zero model calls. Source changes invalidate
   old completed evidence and require fresh execution. New intent needs admission.

The second validation cannot establish the first. Shorter visible output does
not establish reduced reasoning effort. The primary comparison is cumulative
thinking tokens and turns to the first correct answer, with total input/output
usage and Scheme work reported alongside it. Output tokens already include
reasoning tokens; do not double count. Missing usage is unknown, not zero.
Unverified trajectories are censored, not inexpensive successes.

The desired 40–50% reduction remains a hypothesis. None of the completed pilots
below establishes a sustained benefit of that size on an independent complex
workload. Negative results and all historical failures remain in the report.

* Experimental design and quality gates

Use the same question, finite source boundary, model, reasoning effort, raw-fact
access, verification feedback, turn limit and recovery policy in both conditions.
Control reasons over raw facts. The assisted condition additionally has Scheme
checking and retained evidence available inside its reasoning loop. Both can
scan facts first. Do not require a first-round witness query or reject an otherwise
correct assisted answer merely because it did not use Scheme. Report evidence
adoption separately; keep every assigned trajectory in the quality denominator.

A separate ablation is needed to isolate retained cross-round evidence from
execution assistance alone. The present two-arm protocol does not establish
that incremental causal contribution. Likewise, exact-repeat answer caching
is a fair zero-call control; it cannot safely handle changed source facts.

Freeze questions, sources, oracle, producer identities, trial order and analysis
before paid execution. Keep private oracle values and programs out of prompts.
Counterbalance condition order and repeat trials. Two repetitions can expose
variation but cannot establish statistical power. Split by compound structure
and entity anchors, not merely paraphrases. Independently annotated real-world
questions and a larger sample remain necessary for generalization claims.

Report exact answer-set agreement, scope-correct witnesses, success rate, first
verified turn, cumulative reasoning/input/output tokens, provider usage, query
and scan counts, local execution time, and all repair costs. Report requested
and resolved model separately where available. Prefix caching is distinct from
Scheme semantic reuse. Price estimates are sensitivity calculations, not invoices
or end-to-end machine costs. Retain empty, rejected, failed and timed-out cases.

* Compound questions replacing the small capability probes

The former 12-actor tasks are mechanism regressions. The new executable workload
has four compound questions, each with 96 opaque Actor identifiers, 32 Films,
8 Directors and 4 Countries. It includes shared dependencies, directed cycles,
multiple nationalities, competing witnesses, two selected countries and four
target actors. Source order is shuffled independently of semantic IDs.
Increasing entity count alone is insufficient: the new structures combine
four-edge walks, recursive closure, same-film joins, scoped negation, global
anti-existence, branch union and universal target coverage.

** Four-hop shared witness

#+begin_quote
Return actor x iff there is a directed mentor walk of EXACTLY FOUR edges
x->p->q->r->z, and z and an actor t in target appear in the SAME film f whose
director has at least one selected_country. That supporting film f must not be
banned_film. In addition, x must have NO appearance in ANY banned film.
Intermediate actors may repeat; x need not act in f. Nationality belongs to
the director, not the actors. All joins in this witness must use the same f,
director and t. Use only the supplied finite relations. Return unique actor identifiers.
#+end_quote

** Recursive clean bridge

#+begin_quote
Return actor x iff x reaches an actor z through ONE OR MORE directed mentor
edges, and z and a target actor t appear in the SAME film f whose director has
a selected_country; f must not be banned. Neither x nor z may appear in ANY
banned film. Paths may traverse banned-film actors in intermediate positions:
the actor-wide exclusion applies ONLY to x and z. Cycles are allowed; a
zero-edge identity path is not sufficient. Use only the supplied finite relations.
Return unique actor identifiers.
#+end_quote

** Scoped union with a recursive branch

#+begin_quote
Return the UNION of two branches. LEFT: x has an EXACTLY FOUR-edge directed
mentor walk to z, where z and a target actor share a film f with a selected-country
director; f is not banned, and x has NO appearance in ANY banned film.
RIGHT: x has a ONE-OR-MORE-edge directed mentor path to a target actor, and x
acts in some film g with a selected-country director, where this supporting g
is not banned. The right branch has NO actor-wide banned-film exclusion: a
different banned appearance must not remove x from RIGHT. The two branches may
have different witnesses. Preserve local and branch exclusion scope before
taking the union. Use only the supplied finite relations. Return unique actor identifiers.
#+end_quote

** Universal positive reachability

#+begin_quote
Return actors x in actor_domain that can reach EVERY actor t in target through
ONE OR MORE directed mentor edges, and that share at least one unbanned film f
with a target actor, where the director of that same f has a selected_country.
This is universal coverage over the entire target relation, NOT existence of
one reachable target. x must have NO appearance in ANY banned film. A target
actor requires a positive-length path to itself; identity alone does not count.
Cycles count as positive paths. For an empty target set the reachability
condition is vacuous, but the coappearance condition still requires a target
witness. Use only the supplied finite relations. Return unique actor identifiers.
#+end_quote

* Scheme, Lean and Quint responsibilities

Scheme owns admission, actual relation execution, source replacement, evidence
publication and invalidation. EvidenceStore publishes completed derived relations
as read-only aliases carrying source/path identity. Later reasoning rounds can
reference those aliases without synthesizing the established rules again.
Same-path repeats use run(0). Source replacement clears published aliases and
invalidates their consumers; retained base-source paths are recomputed. The model
cannot alter original facts, define source heads or inject derived rows.

Lean proves conditional equivalence and reuse obligations. Quint independently
computes finite update/fault observations. Matching actual Scheme replay is
required: a foreign-model pass alone does not qualify the implementation.
Existing formal scope includes joins, exclusion, materialized equivalence and
conditional subgoal extension. It does not prove hidden thinking, natural-language
intent equivalence, arbitrary compound compilation, C memory safety or model savings.
The new compound oracle checks do not silently extend those proof claims.

* Integration and transport

The public C ABI is =bindings/c/ascent.h= and =interface/engine-api.ss=;
=python/src/ascent_engine= connects through CFFI. Python owns provider byte IO;
Scheme owns relation truth, admission, sessions, execution and updates.
Normal package compilation produces the Scheme modules; the Python package
links the compiler-derived closure into an engine library. There is no
movie-specific Scheme build command. Reusing the initialized runtime removes
repeated local startup, not remote inference latency.

The qualified subset is finite typed relations, constants, variables, positive
atoms, recursion and stratified negation. Arbitrary host predicates, aggregates
and callbacks are unsupported. Runtime/session lifetime, buffer ownership,
thread affinity, error propagation and cancellation require explicit handling.
Qualification includes UTF-8, duplicate keys, transactional rejection, stale
handles, thread affinity and terminal shutdown. Producer/runtime hashes bind
identities; they are not source-to-binary refinement proofs.

** Current implementation changes and remaining evidence

The current protocol permits both first-round fact scans and checked claims in
the assisted condition. It permits unary checked claims, not only artificially
required multi-column subproblem witnesses. Correct final answers are judged
by the independent oracle, without an evidence-use prerequisite. Evidence
adoption is recorded separately. Each query result still retains source/path
identity and published aliases for subsequent thinking. Full-question reasoning,
not mandatory subproblem-first planning, is the experimental object.

Actual Scheme tests verify retained aliases, multi-stage extension, repeat hits,
withdrawal rejection and rebuilding. The prior qualification passed 15 focused
checks plus finite Lean/Quint/Scheme correspondence. Those checks establish
mechanism behavior, not a real-model benefit of cross-round alias composition.
The new compound plan first checks original/withdrawn oracle parity; paid model
results must be reported separately when completed. Local frozen-library
qualification is distinct from fresh-source Ubuntu CI. macOS CI is not a gate
for continuing the main research line.

* Research grounding and next closure criteria

[[https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/miao26e.html][LLM-MatLogic]] motivates scope exclusion before witness projection and
separation of specification versus execution failures; its simple subgraph
movie examples are regressions, not sufficient main evidence here.
[[https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37233][DatalogBench]] motivates execution against held-out inputs and mutation-checked
oracles; the complete benchmark is not reproduced here.
[[https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05752][PUG]] motivates relevant why/why-not support queries; this implementation does
not reproduce the full provenance model. These are design inputs rather than
claims that every paper mechanism has been implemented.

Before each batch, read retained reflection and turn observed admission,
transport, scope, execution and evidence-use gaps into executable regressions.
Keep original failures. The closed-loop claim requires observed evidence use
BEFORE later reasoning, matched first-correct trajectories, preserved quality,
all failed work charged, and repeated compound evaluations. Exact-repeat reuse
and source-update safety are reported separately. Budget is not the present
bottleneck; task complexity, intent verification and coverage determine the
strength of the conclusions.
